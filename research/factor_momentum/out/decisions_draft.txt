Decisions fixed 2026-10-21, after the coverage/code checks (out/checks_old.txt) and BEFORE any hold return, book
return, baseline return or judge output was computed.

1. Correlation matrix = cosine of each stock's return vector standardized by its own mean and sd (ddof 0) over its
   finite days, missing days set to 0, rows scaled to unit norm (c2eval's convention): diagonal exactly 1,
   eigenvalues sum to n. Log returns of export closes (gap day 2011-02-17 forward-filled), no bad-print filter.
2. Eigenvectors from numpy.linalg.eigh, reordered by descending eigenvalue; components 2-21 kept.
3. Factor daily returns in sample (t-251..t): w_k . simple daily return, missing return = 0 (a daily-rebalanced
   constant-weight portfolio). 12-1 past return = sum of those over t-251..t-21 (231 days); 1-month = sum over
   t-20..t; vol = sd over all 252 days x sqrt(252). Out of sample the hold is buy-and-hold from close t to close
   t+21: return = sum_i W_i (P_i(t+21)/P_i(t) - 1). The in-sample and out-of-sample definitions differ
   (daily-rebalanced vs buy-and-hold); immaterial for a sign and a vol scale.
4. A stock that leaves the export or has a missing close during the hold keeps its last close: zero return from
   there (forward-filled closes).
5. Costs: 5 bps x sum |new weight - drifted old weight| at each formation; the first formation pays the build.
6. The book (and every baseline) starts at the first formation with a universe (2004-12-31), so the first 2006 hold
   pays real turnover.
7. Universe: the judge-like liquid set needs 756 days of history, and the export holds only about 300 stocks before
   2005, so the set has 298-302 stocks until 2007-11 and 500 from 2007-12-03. Test 1 keeps the plan's "2006-2019":
   formations dated 2006-01-01..2019-12-31 (the first is 2006-02-01); 2006-02..2007-11 runs on the ~300-stock set
   (the whole set, as it has fewer than 500). The 500-stock part 2007-12..2019 is reported beside it. (Same choice
   as the loners experiment.)
8. Baseline (a): plain 12-1 = ln(close[t-21]/close[t-252]) (stockai's momentum_scores, copied; checked identical),
   decile long-short ($1/$1, round(10% n) per leg, equal weight) over the universe stocks with a finite score.
9. Baseline (b): each universe stock's twin at the formation (c2eval.twins fund_row at rebal k = t); the twin's
   12-1 = sum of the fund's daily store-close log returns over t-251..t-21, a move beyond +-50% or a missing day
   counting 0. Stocks without a twin are dropped. Tie groups straddling a decile edge share the remaining slots
   (fractional equal weights). (b) counts at a formation only if >= 80% of the universe has a twin and there are
   >= 10 distinct twins: true from 2006-02-01 on (before that every twin is QQQ).
10. Test 1: mean NET monthly (21-day hold) return of the book, formations 2006-01-01..2019-12-31, plain t of the
    non-overlapping holds; pass t >= 2.5.
11. Test 2: OLS of the book's NET hold returns on (a)'s and (b)'s NET hold returns, Test 1's formations; intercept
    > 0 with plain OLS t >= 2. Newey-West (3 lags) and the gross version are reported as checks only.
12. Test 3: "same sign" = the plan's direction (positive: factor momentum pays), net mean, formations 2020-01-01..
    the last whose hold ends inside the old export; and the unseen holds (new export) formed on/after 2025-06-27
    ending by 2026-10-19, book warmed up from 2025-01.
13. Stability: eigenvectors of consecutive formations aligned on the union of their stocks (0 for absent stocks);
    each component 2-21's best |cosine| among the previous formation's top 50, the index it matched, the |cos|
    with the same index, and the subspace retention ||projection onto previous comps 2-21||. How often a sign is
    "long" is NOT reported (the eigenvector sign is arbitrary).
14. Beta: realized = OLS slope of the book's monthly gross return on SPY's buy-and-hold return over the same hold
    (store close); ex-ante = sum_i W_i beta_i, beta_i from simple daily returns over t-251..t (pairwise finite).
15. Judge scores: W_j(t) on days t..(next formation - 1), NaN for stocks outside the book and before the first
    formation; raw weights. Graded rebalance=21, uniform and volatility-matched. Plain 12-1 on the same universe
    is graded the same way for reference (daily signal, latest universe).
