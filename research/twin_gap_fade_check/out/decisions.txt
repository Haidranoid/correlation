Decisions for the intraday-bar check of the twin gap fade. Written 2026-10-21T11:32-05:00, BEFORE any intraday

Items added later carry their own time and say whether any intraday return had been seen.

D1  Positions. The headline book as tested, from rows_new.npz with the original books.py logic (stable argsort of z,
    round(10% x n) per leg, intraday bad-print rows out). Reproduction check (positions.py): on 2026-07-01..2026-10-19
    (77 days) the gross and netted twin costs equal books.books() exactly and daily_new.tsv to its 6 decimals, for
    the all-stocks and the 500-largest books.

D2  Price scale. The store holds Stooq's split- and dividend-adjusted prices as of 2026-10-19; Yahoo intraday with
    auto_adjust=False is split-adjusted only. Before using any Yahoo entry against the store's close, verify on daily
    closes (not intraday returns) whether store_close / Yahoo_close steps at ex-dates. Rule: per symbol and segment
    between Yahoo events (dividend or split ex-dates in or after the window through 2026-10-19), k = median of
    store_close / Yahoo_daily_close over the segment's days (see D2a for days that disagree). Every Yahoo price is
    put on the store's scale as yahoo_price x k, so the exit stays the store's close and the store's open-to-close
    (entry a) is untouched. k comes from closes only, never opens.
D2a A stock-day (or twin-day) is usable for Yahoo entries if |store_close / (k x Yahoo_daily_close) - 1| <= 0.1%.
    Otherwise it is a close disagreement: counted and reported, and positions on it are out of the common set.
D2b The rule's event drop: a stock with a dividend or split ex-date on day t is dropped that day; so is any position
    whose twin has one that day (conservative reading: "the stock dropped on that day" applied to both instruments
    of a position). Counted and reported.

D3  Entry prices. (a) the store's open. (b) the open of Yahoo's first regular-session bar, whatever its timestamp.
    (c) 9:35, (d) 9:45, (e) 10:00, and 9:31 on 1-minute bars: the open of the bar whose timestamp is exactly that
    time. No fallback: a missing bar makes the position missing at that time. The twin is entered at the same moment
    from its own bars, by the same definition. Exit: the store's close (on Yahoo's scale via k); reported alongside:
    exit at the close of Yahoo's last bar with timestamp < 16:00 (any 16:00 bars are noted, not used).
D3a Bars are filtered to 09:30 <= time < 16:00 America/New_York whatever prepost says; timestamps are checked to be
    America/New_York.

D4  Windows. A 5-minute window day = a store day in the book where SPY's 5-minute bars run from 09:30 through 15:55
    (America/New_York) with all 78 bars. The 1-minute window: SPY's 1-minute bars run 09:30 through 15:59 (390 bars).
    This drops half-days and a partial first day of Yahoo's range.

D5  Common position set per window: positions whose stock and twin both have every bar the window's entry times need
    (first bar, 9:35, 9:45, 10:00, and 9:31 on 1-minute), a usable close (D2a), and no event flag (D2b). All entry
    times, (a) included, are priced on this set, so paired differences are on identical positions. Legs are the
    mean over the set's positions in each leg; a day with an empty leg is skipped. (a) is also reported on all
    positions (= daily_new.tsv) and on all positions with bars.

D6  Costs for the net figures: the plan's headline costs at every entry time: 2 x 10 bps entry penalty plus 2 bps per
    side on the twin legs netted across stocks (books.fund_cost on the common set's positions). Net does not decide
    the verdict.

D7  Verdict quantities, on the 5-minute window, all-stocks book, gross: share kept = mean over days of the 9:35 gross
    spread / mean over the same days of the (a) gross spread, both on the common set. t = plain t of the daily 9:35
    gross spread. Real: share >= 0.5 and 9:35 mean > 0 with t >= 2. Opening-print effect: share < 0.25. Unresolved:
    anything else. Paired differences: daily (X - a) gross, mean and plain t.

D8  Variant: z_v = (log(P1 x k / C_prev) - beta x log(P1f x kf / Cf_prev)) / sd, where P1 is Yahoo's first-bar open
    (put on the store's scale with k), C_prev the store's previous close, and sd the trailing sd recovered in
    positions.py (it reproduces the rows' z to 5e-5). Ranked set: the day's ranked rows that have a first-bar open
    for stock and twin and pass D2a/D2b; legs round(10% x n) of that set. Priced at every entry time like the main
    book (positions then need the same bars as in D5).

D9  Fetch. 5-minute (period 60d) and daily (auto_adjust=False, actions=True) bars for the window's whole ranked set
    and their twins: the variant needs every ranked stock's first-bar open, and over 2026-07..10 the positions
    already include 2,421 of the 2,453 ranked stocks. 1-minute bars (7-day pieces within the last 30 days) only for
    the stocks and twins in positions (all, 500 and variant books) on 1-minute window days, after the 5-minute pass.

D10 Open agreement: on the 5-minute window's position stock-days of the all-stocks book (each stock-day once),
    pairs store open vs Yahoo daily open, store open vs Yahoo first-bar open, Yahoo daily open vs first-bar open;
    share with |diff| > 1 cent and with |diff| > 0.5%. The store open is compared on Yahoo's scale (store_open / k).
    The 1-cent test is reported on stock-days with k = 1 (no event after the day through 2026-10-19; the store is
    then raw); the 0.5% test on all. Split by exchange (store's: nyse, nasdaq, nysemkt) and by dollar volume (the
    100 most traded, 101-500, the rest; build.py's in100 / in500 flags).

D11 Coverage = positions in the common set / positions in the book on the window's days, per window and book; also
    positions with any bars / positions.
