Decisions for the overnight auctions experiment (experiments/+54.1-overnight-auctions.md).
Written 2026-10-21T14:11-05:00, BEFORE any signal or outcome was computed. Only data-quality checks had been run
(divcheck.py: the cent-grid look at dividend adjustment). Items added later carry their own time.

D1  Dividends and splits (data check, divcheck.py). Stooq back-adjusts cumulatively: every earlier open, high, low and
    close of a symbol is multiplied by the same factor and stored to 6 significant digits, so adjusted prices are
    off the cent grid and raw prices are on it. A one-step change-point fit per symbol (a day is off-grid when >= 2 of
    O, H, L, C are) gives the raw boundary B: days < B adjusted, days >= B raw. Splits are always adjusted (they also
    move B). Most dividend payers' boundaries fall in Oct-Nov 2022 in BOTH stores (KO 2022-11-30, XOM 2022-11-14,
    T 2022-10-06, JPM 2022-10-05, O 2022-11-30; SPY, AAPL and MSFT later). So an ex-date overnight is a total return
    (adjusted) through about 2022 and price-only (the open drops by the dividend against an unadjusted previous close)
    from late 2022 on, including all of the unseen months. Share of universe overnights in the raw region: 12-15% in
    2008-2013 (nearly all stocks that never had an adjusted event), 29% in 2019, 48% in 2022, 94-99% in 2023-2025-06,
    99% in 2026. There is no dividend calendar on disk for 2008-2025, so the plan's "drop ex-date overnights if
    unadjusted" cannot be applied to history.
    Headline: the store as is. Sensitivities, fixed now: (a) every test and the money test's average also over
    2008-2022 only, and Test 3 split into 2020-2022 and 2023-2025-06; (b) in 2026-06-01..2026-10-19 (unseen months,
    raw store) the Yahoo daily file on disk (twin_gap_fade_check y1d.csv: dividends and splits) gives ex-dates for its
    symbols: drop ex-date overnights of the stock or its twin (signal and outcome) and report the change in the unseen
    book and account as the measured size of the bias, with coverage. The "payer" flag (an adjusted region exists) is
    not used as a drop rule: split adjustment also takes prices off the grid.

D2  Timing. Signal day t: the decision is made before the 15:50 cutoff, so S(t) may use opens through t and closes
    through t-1. The universe, twin, beta (twin) and the SPY beta for day t all come from the rebalance
    k = kidx(rebal, t-1), i.e. rebal[k] <= t-1, whose inputs end at that rebalance's close (asserted in the code).
    checks.py verifies it: for sampled t, overwrite every stock and fund close (and high, low) from column t on and
    every open from t+1 on with garbage, recompute, and require S(:, t) bit-identical.

D3  Overnight for the signal, o(d) = log(open_d / close_{d-1}) on consecutive export days, from the store. Missing if
    a price is missing or <= 0, |o| > 0.5 (night and day's bad-print rule), d before 2008-01-02, or the open equals
    the previous close (stale open). The twin gap fade's headline did not drop stale opens; its post-hoc check
    defined them as open = previous close or open = close and found no change. Here the plan says a stale open is not
    a real fill, so open = previous close is dropped; open = close is not usable for the signal (it needs day t's
    close). Same rule for the twin fund's overnight. The rule uses only open_d and close_{d-1}, so x(d) is the same
    whether d is today or an earlier day.

D4  x_i(d) = o_i(d) - beta_i * o_twin(d), with the twin and beta of rebalance k = kidx(rebal, t-1) applied to all 21
    overnights d = t-20..t. S_i(t) = mean of the present x over those 21, at least 17 present. Raw version:
    S_raw = the same mean of o_i (stock only), at least 17 present.

D5  Universe on day t: the judge-like liquid set at rebal[k] (night and day's universe(), copied), with a twin at k
    (fund_row >= 0), minus c2eval.excluded at k; ranked set = universe members with a finite S(t). The raw version
    uses the same universe and additionally needs a finite SPY beta. 500 largest: night and day's rule (top 500 by
    63-day median dollar volume at rebal[k]); deciles re-ranked inside it. A day needs >= 50 ranked stocks.

D6  Outcome position-day (exit day d = t+1): the stock's overnight on d valid by D3's rule, plus night and day's leg
    rule on d (|intraday log| <= 0.5, not open = close = high = low); same for the hedge (twin, or SPY in the raw
    version). Invalid position-days are dropped from that day's leg means (as night and day). Sensitivity for the
    money test: exits with open_{t+1} = close_t kept at a 0 return instead of dropped.

D7  Book: long the top round(10% x n) by S, short the bottom round(10% x n), equal weight, $1 long and $1 short.
    Position return (long) = (open_d / close_t - 1) - beta * (twin open_d / twin close_t - 1); short = minus that.
    Book gross = mean long - mean short over valid positions. Tug of war: the same positions' intraday leg on d,
    (close_d / open_d - 1) - beta * (twin's same), gross. Raw version: rank S_raw, hedge with SPY's overnight at the
    stock's SPY beta: OLS slope of the stock's daily close-to-close log return (export) on SPY's (store close) over
    the 252 days ending at rebal[k], |r| > 0.5 missing, >= 227 pairs.

D8  Costs: stocks 2 bps per side, both sides daily on $1 long and $1 short = 8 bps a day off the long-short. Hedge
    legs netted across positions per fund each day: 2 x 1 bp x sum_f |net fund weight| (enter at close, exit at
    open). Net = gross - 8 bps - hedge cost. Break-even stock cost per side = (gross - hedge cost) / 4.

D9  Tests: periods by exit day. Test 1: all stocks, 2008-01-01..2019-12-31, mean net daily return, plain t >= 3
    (Newey-West 5 lags reported). Test 2: the 500 largest, 2008-2019, net mean > 0 with t >= 2. Test 3: 2020-01-01..
    2025-06-27 net mean > 0 ("same sign" = the plan's direction, positive); then the unseen months
    2025-06-30..2026-10-19 (new export and store, once, at the end), net mean > 0.

D10 Money test (the plan's own): each day t the 20 highest S of the all-stocks ranked set (stable sort), bought at
    close_t and sold at open_{t+1}, long only, unhedged; the day's return is the mean simple overnight return of the
    valid picks. 1,000 random accounts each draw 20 distinct stocks uniformly from the same day's ranked set, same
    validity handling. numpy default_rng(seed 20261021). Calendar-year returns compound the daily returns by the
    exit day's year: 2008 (from the first signal, late January) .. 2025 (to 06-27). Number = mean over the 18 years of
    (account - median random account), in points, at 0 bps; also at 2 bps per side (4 bps a day). Reported: per-year
    differences, years ahead, se, per-year p (share of random accounts >= the account), years with p < 0.1. Unseen
    months: one period 2025-06-30..2026-10-19 (exit days), compounded. Volatility-matched accounts are not in the
    plan and are not run.

D11 The 9:35 extra (unseen months, at the end): the money account's positions with exit day d in the Yahoo 5-minute
    window (from 2026-07-29, days where SPY has all 78 bars 09:30..15:55). Entry = the store's close_t. Exit at the
    store's open_d vs Yahoo's open of the 09:35 bar (y5m.csv open_0935) for the stock on d. Usable only if the store
    close equals Yahoo's daily close within 0.1% on t and d, and no dividend or split ex-date on d (y1d.csv). Share
    kept = mean over the common set of the 9:35 return / mean of the open return; also per-day means with t, and
    the same in excess of the day's universe mean (the ranked stocks with usable bars), and coverage.

D12 Judge plumbing check: night and day's sanity() copied (5-day reversal, s = -log(c_t / c_{t-5}), rebalance=5),
    saved under overnight_auctions/sanity_reversal5. Expect +11.8 pts/yr, beat random at p < 0.1 in 8 of 18 years.
