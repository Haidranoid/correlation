14:02  After seeing the 5m bars' structure (times, volumes, counts per day), before any return was computed
- Missing bar = absent from Yahoo's output, or a non-finite close. Yahoo leaves out bars with no trades (299
  symbol-days have 77 bars, fewer have less) and does not forward-fill them (no flat bar at the prior close).
  2,109 of 2,424,480 five-minute bars have volume 0, but every one has a price range (SPY, QQQ, NVDA, AAPL among
  them), so volume 0 is Yahoo's volume field failing, not "no trades": those bars count as present. A flat
  volume-0 bar at the previous bar's close would count as missing (none occur).
- Return at bar b = log(close_b / close_{b-1}) within the day, needing both bars present. The 09:30 bar's return
  (from the prior close) is excluded. A full 5m day gives returns at bars 2..78 (77 returns) and 76 lag-1 slots
  (r(k), r(k+1)) inside the day; nothing crosses days. 1m: 389 returns, 388 slots.
- Only bars with 09:30 <= time < 16:00 and UTC offset -04:00 (America/New_York, EDT in this whole window) are kept.
- Full day = SPY has all 78 (5m) or 390 (1m) bars from 09:30. This drops half-days and today's partial day.
- 2% rule: a symbol-day counts only if its last bar's close is within 2% of Yahoo's daily close from the fresh
  daily fetch; a symbol-day without a daily bar is dropped. In-window splits from the daily fetch's stock_splits
  column are listed.
- Split: the full days in date order; selection = the first floor(D/2) days, test = the rest.
- Market adjustment: beta per symbol = OLS slope (with intercept) of its returns on SPY's same-bar returns over
  the selection half, over bars where both exist; adjusted r = r - beta * r_SPY, in both halves. A bar where SPY's
  return is missing gives no adjusted return (never happens on a full day).
- SPY's adjusted return is identically zero (beta on itself = 1), so SPY is out of the adjusted pair set: 519
  symbols, 519 x 518 = 268,842 ordered pairs. SPY stays in the raw same-bar context and as test 2's baseline.
- Lead-lag statistic: exact pairwise-complete Pearson correlation of r_A(k) and r_B(k+1) over the slots where both
  exist, from six masked matrix products (n, Sx, Sy, Sxx, Syy, Sxy). Checked against a direct computation for
  pairs drawn at random, real and shifted. Pairs with fewer than 1,000 bar pairs are left out of every count.
- Null: B's days rotated by s within the half (A's day i with B's day (i+s) mod D_half), on the same adjusted
  returns, for s = 1..20 (all distinct and nonzero for the 5m halves). For the 1m halves (about 10 days each)
  only D_half - 1 distinct nonzero shifts exist, so the 1m null uses s = 1..D_half-1.
- Threshold = 99.9th percentile of |corr| pooled over every pair and every shift. The null count = the mean over
  shifts of the number of pairs above the threshold (about 0.1% of pairs by construction). The number for the
  file name = (selection count / null count - 1) x 100.
- Robustness variant, pre-registered and reported, not graded: each symbol's adjusted returns divided by its own
  same-day sd before correlating. Reason: volatility clusters by day across stocks; in the real alignment the
  volatile days of A and B coincide, which fattens the |corr| tail with no lead-lag, while the day-shift null
  breaks that coincidence. Test 1 could pass for that reason alone; test 2 (out of sample) is the real guard.
- Test 2:
  - Selected pairs: |corr| above the threshold in the selection half (signed). A follower's prediction for bar
    k+1 = sum over its selected leaders of sign(corr) x adjusted r_A(k), over the leaders whose r_A(k) exists
    (a missing leader term is dropped, not zero); no leader present -> no prediction.
  - IC target: the follower's adjusted r_B(k+1) (selection-half beta). Raw r_B(k+1) reported too.
  - Spearman rank IC across followers for each bar with at least 10 followers having a prediction and a target.
  - Mean IC over bars; standard error clustered by day: e = IC - mean, SE = sqrt(sum over days of (sum of e in
    the day)^2) / n_bars. Pass: t >= 3.
  - Baseline: prediction = beta_lag_B x r_SPY(k), beta_lag_B = OLS slope of B's raw r_B(k+1) on SPY's raw r(k)
    over the selection half. Its IC is computed on the same followers at the same bar as the lead-lag IC; paired
    difference (lead-lag IC - baseline IC) per bar, day-clustered t >= 2.
- Test 3 (money): each bar, long the top tenth and short the bottom tenth of the followers with a prediction and
  a raw return in bar k+1 (round(0.1 x n), at least 1 per side, n >= 10), equal weight, held one bar, in raw
  (actual) returns. Gross = mean over bars of mean(long) - mean(short). Cost: 1 bp x the turnover of the weights
  (long weights +1/n_long, short -1/n_short; a day starts and ends flat), so replacing the whole book costs 4 bp;
  net = gross - cost, must be positive. Break-even cost per side = gross / mean turnover.
- Funds vs stocks: for each cell (fund->stock, stock->stock, stock->fund, fund->fund) the share of its pairs above
  the threshold, against the null's share for the same cell, plus mean |corr|.
- Same-bar context: the distribution of corr(r_A(k), r_B(k)) over pairs, raw and adjusted, selection half.
- Brief items that don't apply: the judge is not used (intraday bars, no daily scores), so the 5-day reversal
  sanity check has no path here; the unseen months don't apply (the plan says so).
