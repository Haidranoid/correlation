# stockGPT picking the week's biggest gainers

**TL;DR:** stockGPT (`-19.8-stockgpt-next-day.md`) was retrained to predict each stock's rank by its return over the next 5 trading days, then graded exactly like LightGBM in the weekly top-10 test (`+7.4-weekly-top10-picks.md`): 700 weeks, 2010–2023. Its top 10 earned 0.29% a week against 0.26% for the average stock: +0.03 points a week (t = 0.4), about 1.5 points a year. It mostly learned to pick calm stocks (its scores correlate −0.52 with volatility), which rarely make the week's biggest gainers. Over the whole list there is a faint ranking signal (rank correlation +0.012, t = 2.2), but it doesn't reach the top 10. At 20 bps per trade, $10,000 became $4,818 after 14 years, against SPY's $65,036. Verdict: no edge.

- **How it was built:** the same transformer and inputs as the next-day model. The target is the stock's rank by next-week return, mapped onto a bell curve in 101 bins, and the score is the expected rank. There are three seeds plus a sign-flip null, walk-forward with gaps of at least 5 trading days.
- **The faint signal isn't only volatility:** with the volatility tilt removed, its validation rank correlation was positive in 13 of 14 years, against 9 of 14 for the null.
- **The null's account is understated:** it sat in cash on 258 days with no positive score.
