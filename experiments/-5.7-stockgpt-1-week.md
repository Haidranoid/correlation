# stockGPT picking the week's biggest gainers

**TL;DR:** Can stockGPT, the project's transformer model, pick each week's biggest gainers? Retrained to rank stocks by their next-5-day return, its weekly top 10 beat the average stock by only +0.03 points a week (t 0.4), because it mostly learned to pick calm stocks. After costs of 5 basis points a trade it made −5.7 points a year against SPY. Verdict: no edge.

- **How it was built:** the same transformer and inputs as the next-day model. The target is the stock's rank by next-week return, mapped onto a bell curve in 101 bins, and the score is the expected rank. There are three seeds plus a sign-flip null, walk-forward with gaps of at least 5 trading days.
- **The faint signal isn't only volatility:** with the volatility tilt removed, its validation rank correlation was positive in 13 of 14 years, against 9 of 14 for the null.
- **The null's account is understated:** it sat in cash on 258 days with no positive score.
- **Calm stocks:** its scores correlate −0.52 with volatility, and calm stocks rarely make the week's biggest gainers.
- **SPY** turned $10,000 into $65,036 over the same 14 years.
- **The files:** the next-day model is `-19.8-stockgpt-next-day.md`; the weekly top-10 test and its LightGBM row are `+7.4-weekly-top10-picks.md`.
