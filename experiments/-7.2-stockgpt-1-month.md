# stockGPT picking the month's biggest gainers

**TL;DR:** stockGPT (`-19.8-stockgpt-next-day.md`) was retrained to predict each stock's rank by its next-month return (21 trading days). It was then graded exactly like LightGBM in the monthly top-10 test (`+18.6-monthly-top10-picks.md`): 162 months, 2010–2023. Its top 10 earned 0.68% a month, *less* than the average stock's 1.03%: −0.35 points a month (t = −1.7), about −4 points a year. Almost none of its picks ever made the month's 10 biggest gainers (0.01 a month), because what it learned is "pick calm stocks". Its scores correlate −0.43 with volatility on average, and −0.96 in 2022. Its validation numbers were no evidence of skill either: a sign-flip null reached the same validation score through the same volatility tilt. At 20 bps, $10,000 became $15,796 after 14 years, against $230,343 for LightGBM and $65,036 for SPY. Verdict: no edge.

- **How it was built:** the same transformer and inputs as the next-day model. The target is the stock's rank by next-month return, mapped onto a bell curve in 101 bins, and the score is the expected rank. There are three seeds plus a sign-flip null, each year trained only on earlier data with gaps of at least 21 trading days. At a one-day horizon this code reproduces the next-day runs byte for byte.
- **Weak training:** 16 of the 42 models kept their first checkpoint.
- **A next attempt** (for example, early stopping on a measure with the volatility tilt removed) would be a new experiment, decided before seeing its results.
