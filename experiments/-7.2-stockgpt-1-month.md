# stockGPT picking the month's biggest gainers

**TL;DR:** Can stockGPT, the project's transformer model, pick each month's biggest gainers? Retrained to rank stocks by next-month return, its top 10 did −0.35 points a month against the average stock (t −1.7), because it learned to pick calm stocks. After costs of 5 basis points a trade it made −7.2 points a year against SPY. Verdict: no edge.

- **How it was built:** the same transformer and inputs as the next-day model. The target is the stock's rank by next-month return, mapped onto a bell curve in 101 bins, and the score is the expected rank. There are three seeds plus a sign-flip null, each year trained only on earlier data with gaps of at least 21 trading days. At a one-day horizon this code reproduces the next-day runs byte for byte.
- **Weak training:** 16 of the 42 models kept their first checkpoint.
- **A next attempt** (for example, early stopping on a measure with the volatility tilt removed) would be a new experiment, decided before seeing its results.
- **Calm stocks:** its scores correlate −0.43 with volatility on average, and −0.96 in 2022.
- **Its validation numbers were no evidence of skill:** the sign-flip null reached the same validation score through the same volatility tilt.
- **SPY** turned $10,000 into $65,036 over the same 14 years.
- **The files:** the next-day model is `-19.8-stockgpt-next-day.md`; the monthly top-10 test and its LightGBM row are `+18.6-monthly-top10-picks.md`.
