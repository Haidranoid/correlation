# Peer momentum (signal family C)

**TL;DR:** This is the published, economically motivated form of lead-lag: stocks whose peers rose last month should rise next month (Ali & Hirshleifer 2020; Cohen & Frazzini 2008). The peers came from the census: each stock's 20 strongest same-day co-movers, the one structure that does persist. The picks beat random guessers in 3 of 18 years (chance gives about 1.8) and averaged 2 points a year *less* than random. The ranking signal came out slightly reversed (rank IC z = −2.79, positive in only 6 of 18 years): stocks whose peers had risen did a little worse. Verdict: no edge. Since about 2016 the published effect has turned into a mild peer reversal, which fits the literature: co-movers share price pressure that reverts (Chen et al. 2019).

## Rule, fixed in advance

- **Peers:** each stock's 20 strongest positive same-day correlates (census variable) over the 3 years before the holdout.
- **Score:** the peers' mean log return over the last 21 trading days.
- **Account:** top 20 stocks, rebalanced every 21 trading days. Same $10,000 account, turnover-matched random guessers and benchmarks as the census picks (`-11.4-census-picks.md`).
- **No parameter variants were tried,** so the result isn't the best of several.

## Results

**Holdout 2024-06-28..2025-06-27:**
- Picks +12.7% (SPY +13.6%, QQQ +14.3%). Random median +7.8%; 30% of random guessers did as well (p = 0.30).
- +11.4% at 5 bps and +7.7% at 20 bps: monthly trading keeps costs small.
- Rank IC t −0.42; long-short t 0.20.

**Walk-forward, 18 years (2007–2025):**
- **Money:** beat random at p < 0.1 in 3 of 18 years (binomial p ≈ 0.27).
- **Ranking:** rank IC positive in only 6 of 18, **combined z = −2.79**, a peer *reversal* in residual terms concentrated in 2016–2023.
- **Long-short:** in actual returns 0.51 bps/day, positive in 10 of 18 years, z = 0.15.

**In the forward log:** peer-momentum picks are logged with each data update. The first real forward row (picks as of 2026-10-19: KRUS, M, GDYN, TXRH, TPR, …) matures around 2026-11-17.

## What it means

- Economic-link momentum is documented with *economic* links (customers, shared analysts, text peers). Same-day co-movement peers are a different thing.
- Chen, Chen, Chen & Li (2019) show that a correlation-peer signal is mostly own-stock reversal plus one-month industry momentum.
- The reversed sign here is the basis of the "bystanders" idea (`-7.5-bystanders.md`): co-movement without a business reason gets undone.
