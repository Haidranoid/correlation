# v1's idea tested properly: pair lead-lag on a holdout year

**TL;DR:** v1's core idea was that one stock's daily moves predict another's 1–5 days later. Here it was tested the honest way: learn on 2021–2024, then trade the unseen year that followed. The strongest of 23 million leader/target/offset combinations were no stronger than the same search produces by pure chance. In the holdout year their direction was right 52.3% of the time, against 49.6% for random pairs. A $10,000 account made 15.6% before costs (SPY 13.6%), but 24.5% of random-guess accounts did as well (p = 0.245), and at 5 bps per trade it lost 8.0%. The method itself works: it finds the known effect of large caps leading small caps by a day. Verdict: no edge for pairs of single stocks.

## Design

Every decision was made from training data only.
- **Training and holdout:** training 2021-06-28 to 2024-06-27 (755 days); holdout 2024-06-28 to 2025-06-27 (250 days).
- **Universe:** 2,155 US common stocks. Price ≥ $5, median dollar volume ≥ $5M, traded on ≥ 95% of days. ETFs and test tickers excluded, bad prints repaired.
- **Returns:** daily log returns minus each stock's beta to the equal-weight market (betas from training), standardized and clipped at ±5 sd.
- **Search:** lead-lag correlation at offsets of 1 to 5 trading days, one matrix product per offset: 23.2 million leader/target/offset combinations. The top 2,000 are kept.
- **Chance level:** the same search with the targets' timeline shifted.
- **Account:** each holdout day, a stock's score is the sum over stored pairs of r × the leader's move k days earlier. Buy the top 20 at the close, equal weight, rebalanced daily.
- **Benchmarks:** SPY, QQQ, an equal-weight hold of all candidates, and 1,000 random guessers under the same rules and costs.

**What it means:**
- **At portfolio level, lead-lag exists:** big caps lead small caps.
- **Pairs of single stocks are too noisy to use.**
- **Costs:** daily rebalancing of 20 names costs about 25% a year at 5 bps per trade, which no signal this size can cover.

## Caveats

- **One run:** one year and one configuration (daily horizon, offsets 1–5, top 2,000).
- **Survivorship:** the data has survivors only. That flatters all long-only rows equally, so the comparison with random guessers stays fair.
- **Timing:** trading at the same close that computes the signal is slightly optimistic.

This led to the census (`+2.0-correlation-census.md`), which repeats the test for every pair, offset, move length and correlation period.
