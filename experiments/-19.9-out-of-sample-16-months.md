# The 16 months the census never saw

**TL;DR:** A fresh Stooq download (prices through 2026-10-19) gave 329 trading days after the census's last day, 2025-06-27. The census, its forecasts and the reversal control were run on that unseen stretch, unchanged. The census's picks made +21.0% before costs, against +20.3% for the median turnover-matched random account (p = 0.475) and +24.6% for SPY; at 5 bps they made +0.4%. The 61 large-cap forecasts got the direction right 51.7% of the time, against 50.0% expected (95% range 47.9–55.5%). The 5-day reversal control made +23.0% against +19.3% for random (p = 0.41). Verdict: no edge on genuinely new data, the same answer as the walk-forward.

## Results (2025-06-30..2026-10-19, 329 trading days)

**Census picks** (89,022 pairs from the 4 correlation periods that ended before the holdout; 2,147 stocks can be picked; 312 of the census's stocks have since left the data):

| | Final | Return | Random guessers |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPY | $12,465 | 24.6% | |
| QQQ | $13,107 | 31.1% | |
| All 2,147 candidates | $12,077 | 20.8% | |
| Picks, 0 bps | $12,105 | 21.0% | median $12,025; p = 0.475 |
| Picks, 5 bps | $10,042 | 0.4% | p = 0.482 |
| Picks, 20 bps | $5,729 | −42.7% | p = 0.489 |

- **Prediction quality:** hit rate 51.0% of 6,563 picks; IC +0.0030 (t = 2.01).
- **Long-short:** −0.43 bps/day (t = −0.26).

**Forecasts** (61 large caps, 21-day forecasts every 21 trading days from 2025-06-27):
- **Direction:** right 51.7% (473 of 915).
- **Correlation of predicted with actual:** +0.014 pooled, −0.021 per stock (26 of 61 positive).
- **Size:** predicted moves average 0.06%, against actual moves of 6.36%.

**Reversal control (5-day, weekly):**
- **Picks:** +23.0% against random +19.3%, p = 0.41.
- **Long-short:** 10.15 bps/day gross (t = 0.95), with 85% of the book turned over each week.

## Note

The cluster lead-lag family (`-4.5-cluster-lead-lag.md`) was also run on these months. With the paper's settings: −4.4% against +11.0% random. On all exchanges: +49.5% against +30.5% random, p = 0.185.
