# LightGBM on actual next-day returns

**TL;DR:** Can a LightGBM model trained on actual next-day returns pick tomorrow's winners? For one day only: its top 20 beat random accounts in 10 of 14 years (score-to-return rank correlation +0.021, z 9.2), but in 4 when a day late. It replaces 84% of its names daily, so it makes +5.8 points a year over SPY at costs of 5 basis points a trade but loses 35.7% a year at 20. Verdict: real, too short-lived to pay.

## Results (2010–2023)

| Version | IC, actual returns (z) | Years beating random (0 / 5 / 20 bps) | Mean yearly return (0 / 5 / 20 bps) | Random median (0 / 5 / 20 bps) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily | +0.0210 (9.15) | 10 / 10 / 10 (11 / 11 / 11 volatility-matched); a day late 4 / 4 / 4 | +49.2% / +20.9% / −35.7% | +13.4% / −7.8% / −50.5% |
| Weekly | +0.0145 (2.71) | 6 / 6 / 6; a day late 7 / 7 / 7 | +26.3% / +20.5% / +4.6% | +13.4% / +8.2% / −5.8% |
| 5-day reversal rule, weekly (for comparison) | +0.0145 (3.05) | 6 / 6 / 6 | +27.9% / +22.0% / +5.8% | +13.4% / +8.2% / −6.0% |
| SPY | | | +15.0% | |

- **The daily compounded figure misleads.** It shows +14,391% at 0 bps, mostly from 2019–20 (+103.7%) and 2020–21 (+196.1%). The mean yearly return is the fairer summary.
- **How fast it decays:** IC +0.033 for the next day, +0.021 the day after, +0.016 five days out. Part of close-to-close reversal is bid-ask bounce: a close printed at the bid looks like a drop that recovers, and a real trader can't capture it.
- **Unlike the 5-day rule, it didn't fade at the end:** weekly, it beat random in 2021 and 2023, though not in 2022.
- **What it resembles:** +0.35 correlation with stockGPT, −0.36 with volatility, +0.23 with 12-1 momentum, −0.24 with today's move.
- **Where it came from:** the same LightGBM as `-4.9-lightgbm-next-day.md`, trained on each day's ranking of actual next-day returns instead of correlation2's census variable. It was added during verification, after the first results were seen, so it is an extra rather than a planned test.
- **The strongest daily signal in the project:** its daily IC was positive in all 14 years, 2010–2023.
- **Turnover:** the daily top 20 replaces 84% of its names every day. Random accounts that trade as much lose 50.5% a year at 20 bps, so it beats random and still loses money.

## The 2021 tripwire

It crossed the large-cap leakage limit in one year: 2021, +0.0503 against 0.05. A separate agent tried to prove a leak and couldn't:
- every score refits bit for bit;
- garbling data after the decision day changes nothing;
- each year's model is unchanged when data after its last validation outcome is garbled.

2021 is the one year in 21 where low volatility alone crosses 0.05 on large caps. Given this model's spread from year to year, one year in 14 crossing the line without a leak is about 10–30% likely.
