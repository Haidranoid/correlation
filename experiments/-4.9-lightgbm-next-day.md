# LightGBM on correlation2's daily move, next day

**TL;DR:** Can a standard machine-learning model, LightGBM, pick tomorrow's winners? Trained on the project's risk-adjusted daily move, its top 20 beat random accounts by +14.4 points a year before costs, but it had no skill at ranking actual returns: it had learned a part of tomorrow's adjusted move that is already known today. After costs of 5 basis points a trade it made −4.9 points a year against SPY. Verdict: no real edge.

**How its top 20 win anyway:**
- Its scores lean against last year's winners (−0.68 with 12-1 momentum), toward this week's losers (+0.21 with the 5-day reversal rule), and toward volatile stocks (picks' volatility decile 6.0, against 4.5 for random accounts).
- Its long-short is 5.49 bps/day (z = 2.99), even though its overall IC is negative.

### Notes

- **The model:** stockai's planned machine-learning baseline, gradient-boosted trees on 21 simple price features, one model per test year 2010–2023, trained to predict correlation2's next-day census variable.
- **Trained on actual returns,** the same model did much better before costs (`+5.8-lightgbm-next-day-actual-returns.md`).

## The census-unit artifact (k)

- **Why part of tomorrow is known.** correlation2's variable is today's residual minus its trailing-year mean, divided by its trailing volatility. So part of tomorrow's value, k = −(mean ÷ sd) of the last 252 days' residuals, is known today.
- **k scores well in census units and badly in money.** As a score on its own, k gets a census-unit IC of +0.055 at one day (z = 20.4), +0.13 at five days and +0.24 at 21. Its IC in actual returns is −0.0145.
- **This model learned k.** Its scores correlate +0.77 with k. With k removed from the outcome, its census IC drops from +0.0556 to −0.0007.
- **Its near-zero IC in actual returns is two parts cancelling:** −0.0117 from its lean on k, against +0.0095 from its volatility tilt and the rest of its ranking.
- **The census's 21-day reversal control was also mostly k:** census IC +0.088, of which +0.071 is k.
- **The rule since:** grade in actual returns, or against the census outcome minus k.
