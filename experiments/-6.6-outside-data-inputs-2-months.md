# Outside data as extra model inputs, two months

**TL;DR:** This is the same test as `+20.9-outside-data-inputs-1-week.md`, run on the two-month top-10 test (`+3.6-two-month-top10-picks.md`). With outside data the picks earned −0.03 points per two-month period against the average stock, about −0.2 points a year, against +0.91 for the stock-only model. Over comparable stocks the result went from −0.22 to −0.59 points. The $10,000 account at 20 bps fell from $31,176 to $15,990. The control with the outside series shifted four years out of line, so they mean nothing, did best of all: $115,205. That shows how far these accounts swing on noise. Verdict: no improvement.
