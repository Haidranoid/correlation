# Picking the biggest gainers two months out (stockai-2m)

**TL;DR:** Can a LightGBM model pick the next two months' 10 biggest gainers? Not reliably: the picks beat the average stock by +0.9 points a period (t 0.7), all of it from the 5 best of 78 periods, and trailed comparable stocks. That averages +3.6 points a year over SPY after costs, but the gains are lumpy: compounded, $10,000 became $31,176 against SPY's $65,036, and costs explain only about 2 points a year of that. Verdict: no edge.

- **Without the 5 best periods** the picks trail the average stock by 1.3 points a period (t = −1.2).
- **Subsets:**
  - Without collapsed stocks: −0.37 points (t = −0.4).
  - Among the 500 largest: +1.8 (t = 1.7), but the null gets +1.1 there.
- **Collapsed share:** collapsed stocks were 45% of the picks. Their wild outcomes are why the top 10 don't capture the whole list's small ranking edge.
- **The noisiest of the three horizons:** only 78 periods.
