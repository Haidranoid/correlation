# Group breadth thrust: when most of a group breaks out together, does it keep going?

**TL;DR:** When most stocks in a co-moving group climb above their 50-day average within days, does the group keep beating the market? Slightly: over 2006–2019 the next 21 days added +15.5 basis points (t 1.3; the bar was +50 at t ≥ 3). The 20-stock test account beat SPY by +2.6 points a year after costs, not significantly: it is a bet on two or three industries at a time. Verdict: no edge.

## Nearest prior work

| Work | What it found |
|---|---|
| Zweig 1986, *Winning on Wall Street* | The market breadth thrust (10-day average advance share rising from below 40% to above 61.5%) has preceded strong market rallies; a small number of events |
| Moskowitz & Grinblatt 1999 | Industry momentum, the baseline effect for any group signal |
| `+5.1-co-movement-tools.md` | This project: the groups map's groups are stable and real |

**Novelty check (2026-10-21, a separate search; it changed no rules):** not found.
- **Zaremba, Szyszka, Karathanasopoulos & Mikutowski 2021** (*Economic Modelling* 97): breadth predicts market and industry portfolio returns in 64 countries, 1973–2018. There is no thrust trigger, and the industries are standard.
- **No peer-reviewed test of Zweig's thrust was found.** SentimenTrader notes the 61.5% trigger was chosen to exclude past failures, and results get worse at 60%.
- **Zaremba, Umutlu & Maydybura 2020:** predictability across countries and industries has fallen, most in large developed markets.

## Rules (fixed before looking at results)

**Groups:**
- the groups map rebuilt every 21 trading days on a fixed calendar: 30 groups of the 1,500 most-traded stocks, from market-relative correlations over 252 days;
- minus `c2eval.excluded(E, tw)`;
- copy the implementation from an earlier experiment (`research/imposters/groups.py` or the phantom portfolios code) and say which.

**Breadth:** for each group each day, the share of its members (as of the latest refit) whose close is above their own 50-day simple moving average.

**Thrust:**
- breadth was ≤ 0.40 on some day in the last 10 trading days, and is ≥ 0.615 today;
- only the first such day counts; no repeat for the same group within 21 days.

**Trade:** buy the group's members equal weight at the close of the thrust day, hold 21 trading days. Outcome: the members' mean market-relative log return (minus the export's `market`).

**Baselines:**
- **(a) the group's ordinary returns:** its average 21-day market-relative return over all days;
- **(b) momentum-matched non-thrusts:** days when a group's 10-day return falls in the same decile as at thrusts, but breadth didn't thrust.

## Tests, in order (the verdict stops at the first failure; every number is still computed)

1. **2006–2019:** the mean 21-day market-relative return after thrusts, clustered by date. **Pass:** ≥ +50 bps with t ≥ 3.
2. **Beats the momentum-matched non-thrusts:** difference t ≥ 2.
3. **2020–2025-06:** same sign. The 16 unseen months (new export, once, at the end): same sign.

**Money test (the judge):**
- Members of a thrusting group get a score on days t .. t+20. The score is their correlation with the group's average over 252 days, so there are no ties. NaN elsewhere.
- Graded with `rebalance=1`. Uniform and volatility-matched random accounts are both reported.

## The number for the file name

The judge's walk-forward change over random, with uniform random accounts, 0 bps (`c2eval.change_over_random`).

## Ways it can fool us

- **Market rallies:** thrusts bunch together after selloffs, when many groups thrust at once. The outcome is market-relative, and clustering by date accounts for the bunching.
- **Changing groups:** members change at each refit. Breadth uses the latest membership, which is point in time.

### Money test (the judge)

- **Scores:** members of a thrusting group score 1 + their correlation with the group's average over the refit's 252 days, on days d .. d+20.
- **The account:** the judge buys the 20 highest scores, with `rebalance=1`, over the walk-forward 2007-08 .. 2024-25.
- **Cash days:**
  - 287 of the 4,779 decision days from 2006-06-28 had no scored stock. On those days the account holds cash, and so do the random accounts, which hold as many names.
  - 2 days had 1 to 19 scored stocks, so the account held cash for the missing picks.

**Why single years mean little** (a post-hoc diagnostic, reported only, `diag_old.txt`):
- **Few groups at once:** the 20 picks come from 2.3 thrusting groups a day on average, and all 20 come from one group on 26% of days.

- **The honest yardstick** is the spread across years: +7.6 ± 6.2.

## Decisions made while building it

**Before any outcome data:**
- **Groups-map source:**
  - `standardize`, `market_relative`, `kmeans`, `spectral` and `cluster_ols` were copied verbatim, read-only, from `research/phantom_portfolios/pp_common.py`, the numpy port of `together.rs` that phantom portfolios used;
  - `research/imposters/groups.py` is a second port of the same code. The phantom-portfolios one was used, as instructed;
  - only the parameters changed to this plan's: 30 groups of 1,500, not 20 of 1,000.
- **Refit calendar:**
  - refits fall on `c2eval.twins`'s rebalance days (export day 252 + 21k), so `excluded(E, tw)[k]` belongs to refit k;
  - refit k uses closes through its day t0 only, and its groups hold for decisions on t0 .. t0+20.
- **Universe:**
  - excluded stocks are dropped first;
  - then stocks with a return on ≥ 95% of the 252 days, ranked by median dollar volume over them; the top 1,500;
  - simple daily returns, made market-relative with each stock's beta to the equal-weight mean of the 1,500, as in the copied code;
  - seed 0x6E0 + t0, phantom portfolios' convention.
- **Breadth:**
  - the SMA is the mean of the 50 closes ending at d, including d, and needs ≥ 40 of them; "above" is strictly greater;
  - a group's breadth needs a value for ≥ 80% of its members;
  - groups under 10 members never thrust (none occurred).
- **Same members throughout:** breadth on the 10 lookback days and on the day before uses the current refit's members, so a thrust never mixes two memberships.
- **Thrust details:**
  - "the last 10 trading days" is d−10 .. d−1;
  - "only the first such day" means the condition was false on d−1, with the same members;
  - "no repeat within 21 days" means at least 21 trading days since the group's previous thrust;
  - across a refit, a group is the same group as the previous refit's group holding most of its members, and inherits its last thrust day.
- **Outcome:**
  - log close d → d+21 minus the sum of the export's `market` over d+1 .. d+21, averaged over members with both closes, one equal-weight number per thrust;
  - thrusts without a full 21 days are left out of the tests (4 in June 2025, 11 in the unseen months);
  - periods are assigned by thrust date.
- **Clustering:**
  - CR1 by thrust date; the independent dates are the distinct thrust dates;
  - t by 21-trading-day blocks is reported alongside, because outcomes on nearby dates overlap.
- **Baseline (a):**
  - the mean 21-day outcome over every other group-day of the same period;
  - the difference is each thrust minus that mean, t by thrust date with the mean taken as fixed.
- **Baseline (b):**
  - the matching variable is the group's 10-day market-relative log return into d (members' mean minus the market). It is market-relative because the outcome is;
  - deciles come from all group-days of the period;
  - controls are group-days where the thrust condition is false and the same group had no thrust in the 20 days before;
  - the difference is each thrust minus its decile's control mean, t by thrust date;
  - a regression with decile effects, clustered by date on both sides, is reported as a check.
- **Test 2:**
  - requires both (b), the plan's test, and (a), because the plan's TL;DR says a thrust "must beat the group's ordinary returns";
  - test 3 checks the sign of the after-thrust mean.
- **Scores:**
  - 1 + the member's leave-one-out correlation with its group's mean market-relative return over the refit's 252 days. The judge buys only positive scores, and adding 1 keeps the ranking;
  - where two thrusts overlap, the larger score is kept;
  - group labels (the most common twin fund) are for reporting only.
- **Unseen months:**
  - refits on the new export from 2024-06-11, so the 21-day rule has a year of history;
  - thrusts dated 2025-06-30 on, with baselines from the unseen months' own group-days;
  - both random matches were run.

**After seeing universe sizes, before any outcome:**
- **No $5M floor:** together.rs's $5M median dollar-volume floor left 1,063–1,078 stocks in 2008, against the plan's 1,500. The floor was dropped, so the universe is the plan's 1,500 most-traded.
- **No groups before 1,500 qualify:** refits with fewer than 1,500 qualifying stocks make no groups. That is every refit before 2006-03-03, since the export holds about 320 stocks before 2005.

**After results** (reported only):
- **The pick-concentration diagnostic** (`bt_diag.py`), added after seeing the unseen months' +67 points. No rule changed.
