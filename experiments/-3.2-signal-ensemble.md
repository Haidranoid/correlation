# Signal ensemble: do the weak signals add up?

**TL;DR:** Do the project's weak signals add up? Averaged with equal weights, five of them (among them 5-day reversal, momentum and a LightGBM model) rank stocks a little better than any one alone. But a weekly long-short on the average made only 2.4 basis points a week after costs (t = 0.27, 2 needed), and as a stock-picking account it trailed SPY by 3.2 points a year after costs. Verdict: no edge.

| Novy-Marx & Velikov 2016, *RFS* | Buy/hold spreads cut turnover and let some anomalies survive costs |
| Grinold & Kahn, *Active Portfolio Management* | The fundamental law: the information ratio grows with skill × √(number of independent bets) |

**Novelty check (2026-10-21, a separate search; it changed no rules):** partly done, and close.
- **Blitz, Hanauer, Honarvar, Huisman & van Vliet 2023** (*FAJ* 79(4), "Beyond Fama-French Factors: Alpha from Short-Term Signals"), MSCI World 1985–2021, 25 bps a trade:
  - five short-term signals (industry-relative reversal, one-month industry momentum, analyst revisions, seasonality, idiosyncratic volatility), each negative alone after costs;
  - combined with a buy-10/hold-50 buffer, more than 6% a year after costs, including after publication.
- **DeMiguel et al. 2020:** combining signals lets trades net out.
- **Novy-Marx & Velikov 2016:** buffers are the best single cost cutter.
- **Chen & Velikov 2023:** after costs and decay, combinations earn about 20 bps a month.

**Standard practice in quantitative investing, but new for this project,** which had tested every signal alone.

## Rules (fixed before looking at results)

**Universe:** the judge-like liquid set, minus `c2eval.excluded(E, tw)`.

**Components:** each is computed daily from data through the close, then ranked across the universe to [−1, +1], higher meaning expected to rise.
1. 5-day reversal: minus the 5-day market-relative log return.
2. 12-1 momentum: the past 252-day return skipping the last 21. Use the definition in `stockai/stockai/baselines/momentum.py`.
3. Twin residual reversal: minus `c2eval.twin_residual(E, tw, 5)`.
4. Low correlation: minus the 3-day-return correlation with the market over 5 years, within volatility quintiles. Read `experiments/-5.5-betting-against-correlation.md` and copy its definition.
5. stockai's LightGBM on actual returns, where available (2010-06..2024-06).

**Composite:** the plain mean of the available component ranks. Equal weights, no fitting.

**Books:**
- **The test book:** weekly (every 5 trading days, fixed calendar), long the top 10% of the composite, short the bottom 10%. With a buffer: a name stays in its leg until it drops out of the top (bottom) 30%. Costs 5 bps per side on actual turnover.
- **The buffered long-only account:** buy names entering the top 20, hold until they drop out of the top 60, weekly. Reported against SPY and the equal-weight universe.

## Tests, in order (the verdict stops at the first failure; every number is still computed)

1. **2008–2019:** the composite's weekly rank IC in actual returns. **Pass:** t ≥ 3, and higher than every single component's IC t over the same weeks.
2. **The buffered long-short, 2008–2019, net of 5 bps:** mean weekly return with t ≥ 2.
3. **2020–2025-06:** same sign. The 16 unseen months (new export, once, at the end; no LightGBM there, so the composite has four components): same sign.

**Money test (the judge):**
- Scores = the composite every day, graded with `rebalance=5`, no buffer, since the judge can't hold one.
- Uniform and volatility-matched random accounts are both reported.

## The number for the file name

The judge's walk-forward change over random, with uniform random accounts, 0 bps (`c2eval.change_over_random`).

## Ways it can fool us

- **Mostly reversal:** three of the components are reversal-like and correlated. Report the correlations between components; the gain from combining is limited by how alike they are.
- **Survivor-only data:** buying recent losers is flattered. Report the 500-largest version.

## Results (run 2026-10-21)

**What the data allowed.**
- **The universe** first exists on 2006-09-13. It holds about 300 stocks until 2008-02, 850–1,000 in 2009–2012, 1,600 in 2019 and 2,100–2,200 in 2025–26.
- **The composite** has four components before 2010-06-25 and after 2024-06-26 (LightGBM's window), and five in between. The unseen months have four.
- **Weeks:** tests 1 and 2 use 604 weekly decision days in 2008–2019, test 3 uses 275 in 2020–2025-06, and the unseen months 65 (2025-07-01..2026-10-08).

### The unseen months (2025-06-30..2026-10-19)

- **The tests:** see test 3 and the tables above. IC +0.0113 (t 0.86); the buffered long-short made +21.8 bps a week gross and +12.1 net (t 0.40); the long-only account made +46.8% against +25.0% for SPY. In the 500 largest: IC +0.0081 (t 0.49), net −2.9 bps a week.
- **The judge** (single holdout, `rebalance=5`):
  - **The picks made +71.5%** (+61.9% at 5 bps, +36.4% at 20 bps).
  - **Uniform:** the median random account made +18.0%, so +53.5 points over the 16 months; no random account did as well (p = 0.000).
  - **Volatility-matched:** median +35.1%, so +36.4 points, p = 0.045.
  - **Benchmarks:** SPY +24.6%, QQQ +31.1%, all 2,155 candidates +20.7%.
  - **Its ranking measures were weak:** daily rank IC +0.0042 (t 0.30); long-short 3.9 bps a day (t 0.73).
- **What drove it** (a diagnostic added after seeing it, `diag_unseen.py`):
  - **An approximate rebuild of the judge's account** made +80.6% compounded, or 63.4 points as the sum of its weekly returns.
  - **One stock, uniQure (QURE), supplied 20.7 of those points.** It was held when it rose 248% on 2025-09-24.
  - **The five biggest contributors** (QURE, ASTS, RCAT, GFI, AGX) supplied 34.6 points; the other 403 stocks held, 28.9.
  - **How to read it:** one period with one outsized winner isn't evidence of an edge. The weekly tests, which weigh every stock, show only a weak tilt that isn't significant.

## Decisions made while building it

All of these were made on 2026-10-21 **before any return after a decision day was computed or looked at**, unless a line says otherwise. The only data seen before them: universe sizes, component coverage, and the checks of the components against stockai's and BAC's files.

**Universe**
- **The judge-like liquid set** is the judge's own rule (`stockai/judge/src/holdout.rs` `liquid_universe`, with its default `--peer-window 756`), made point in time with windows ending at the decision day d: the census variable `v` finite on ≥ 90% of days d−755..d; the upper median of the finite closes ≥ $5 and of the finite dollar volumes ≥ $5M over d−251..d (upper median as the judge takes it); a finite close on d. Then minus `c2eval.excluded(E, tw)[rebal_index(tw, d)]`. Recomputed every day.
- **Size:** it first exists on 2006-09-13. About 300 stocks through 2008-02, 850–1,000 in 2009–2012, about 1,600 in 2019 and 2,100 in 2025. Test 1 starts in 2008-01 as the plan fixed, so its first two months rank about 300 stocks.

**Components** (each ranked every day among the universe stocks where it is finite, to [−1, +1], ties at their average rank)
- **5-day reversal:** −(ln(close[d] ÷ close[d−5]) − the sum of the export's `market` over d−4..d). The market term is the same for every stock on a day, so the rank is exactly plain 5-day reversal's.
- **12-1 momentum:** `momentum_scores` copied from `stockai/stockai/baselines/momentum.py`, ln(close[d−21] ÷ close[d−252]), over the whole export. Check: it matches stockai's `momentum_12_1.npy` on all 15.4 million cells where both exist (max difference 4.8e−7, float32 storage); the only extra cells are after that file's last column, 2024-06-26.
- **Twin residual reversal:** −`c2eval.twin_residual(E, tw, 5)` (scaled), on the export as it is. The market-wide gap day 2011-02-17 is not filled, so a few weekly decision days around it have no twin residual for most stocks; the composite then averages the other components.
- **Low correlation:** volatility and correlation computed with the code of `research/betting_against_correlation/panels.py`, copied (sd of daily log returns over 252 days with ≥ 120; correlation of overlapping 3-day log returns with the export's `market` over 1,260 days with ≥ 750). Check: identical to that experiment's `panels_old.npz`, cell for cell. Each day the universe stocks with both values are split into volatility quintiles (floor(5 × rank ÷ n), as BAC's money test), and minus the correlation is ranked to [−1, +1] within each quintile.
- **LightGBM:** stockai's `lgbm_ret/scores.npy` as saved (finite from 2010-06-25 to 2024-06-26), ranked among universe stocks. Inside that window it covers the whole universe.
- **Coverage** (`coverage_old.txt`): each component covers ≥ 98% of the universe in every year.

**Composite**
- **The plain mean of the stock's finite component ranks, if it has at least 3** (the plan's "available"). With coverage near 100%, almost every stock averages all of the day's components: four before 2010-06-25 and after 2024-06-26, five in between.
- Not re-ranked: the books order stocks by it, and the judge picks the top 20 (all positive).

**Weekly calendar and returns**
- **Decision days:** export day indices 252 + 5k, a fixed calendar. A position decided at the close of d is held over d+1..d+5.
- **A week's return:** close[d+5] ÷ close[d] − 1 on forward-filled closes (a day without a close earns 0, as in the judge).
- **Periods by decision date:** 2008-01-01..2019-12-31 for tests 1–2 (sub-periods 2008–09, 2010–14, 2015–19); 2020-01-01..2025-06-27 for test 3; the unseen months 2025-06-30..2026-10-19.

**Test 1 (rank IC)**
- **A week's IC:** the rank correlation between the score at d and the week's return, over the universe stocks with a score; a week counts if ≥ 100 stocks have one. t = mean ÷ (sd ÷ √weeks).
- **"Higher than every single component's IC t over the same weeks":** for each component, its IC t and the composite's are computed on the weeks where the component has an IC and on the stocks where both are finite. For LightGBM those are the weeks 2010-06-25..2019-12. The composite's headline t ≥ 3 uses all 2008–2019 weeks.

**Test 2 (the buffered long-short)**
- **Legs:** each week, order the stocks with a composite. The long leg = last week's longs still in the top 30%, plus every stock in the top 10% (round(0.1 n) and round(0.3 n) names). The short leg mirrors it at the bottom. A held stock without a composite is sold.
- **Weights:** equal within each leg, $1 long and $1 short, reset every week.
- **Costs:** 5 bps × Σ|new weight − last week's weight drifted by the week's returns and rescaled to $1 a leg|, charged in the week of the trade; the first week pays the full entry. The book runs continuously from the first decision day of 2008 to the end of the old export.
- **t** = mean ÷ (sd ÷ √weeks) of weekly net returns. **Turnover is reported** as the share of the book traded a week (Σ|Δw| ÷ 4: replacing one name in each leg of m names is 4 ÷ m of Σ|Δw|, i.e. 1 ÷ m of each leg).
- **Also reported, not tested:** the same long-short without the buffer, the same buffered long-short on each component alone, and on the composite without LightGBM.

**Test 3**
- **"Same sign"** means both the composite's mean weekly IC and the buffered long-short's mean net weekly return are positive, in 2020–2025-06 and, separately, in the unseen months.

**The buffered long-only account**
- Each week: last week's holdings still in the top 60 of the composite, plus every stock in the top 20, equal weight (so 20 to 60 names). The same cost rule; the first week buys the whole account.
- **Benchmarks:** SPY (the store's adjusted closes) and the equal-weight universe (every stock with a composite, reset weekly, no costs). Reported: return a year, volatility, the excess over each benchmark with its t, and the CAPM alpha against each.

**The 500 largest**
- The 500 universe stocks with the highest 252-day median dollar volume each week. Every component is re-ranked within them and the composite recomputed; the long-short is rebuilt within them (50 names a leg). Reported, not pass/fail.

**Component correlations**
- The mean over 2008–2019 weeks of each pair's cross-sectional correlation of ranks (LightGBM pairs over its weeks only), and the correlation of the components' weekly IC series.

**Unseen months**
- Everything rebuilt on the new export: universe, components (no LightGBM, so four), composite. `c2eval.twins(E_new)` is read from c2eval's cache. The weekly book starts from cash on the first decision day on or after 2025-06-30.

**Money test**
- The scores file is the daily composite, NaN outside the universe or with fewer than 3 components. The judge picks from its own yearly universe, so the account picks from the overlap. Graded with `rebalance=5`, uniform and volatility-matched random accounts. (This restates the plan and the rules above; it was written up after the run, and nothing new was decided in it.)

**Made after seeing results** (all reported only; none changes a test, a threshold or the verdict)
- **The other four weekly calendars** (`anchors.py`): added because test 1 passed by 0.01 in t. The fixed calendar (252 + 5k) remains the test.
- **Return by tenth and the legs' volatility** (`diag_deciles.py`): added to explain why the composite's long-short is smaller than 5-day reversal's despite a higher IC. Volatility is the 252-day sd from the low-correlation inputs, divided by that week's median across the ranked stocks. It also reads QURE's largest moves on the new export.
- **What drove the unseen months' judge result** (`diag_unseen.py`): an approximation of the judge's account. Candidates are the universe on the day before the holdout; every 5 days it takes the top 20 positive composites and holds them 5 days. Its +80.6% is close to, not equal to, the judge's +71.5%.
- **The new export was run once,** in the fixed order (universe, components, composite, tests, money), with the code unchanged from the old export's run.
- Nothing else was changed after results were seen.
