# Frog in the pan: is momentum built from many small moves stronger?

**TL;DR:** This replicates published work; nothing in the method is new, and what's new is only the test: this project's liquid, survivor-only US stocks over 2006–2025-06 and the 16 unseen months, after the paper's sample except for its last two years (2006–2007). Da, Gurun & Warachka (2014, *RFS*) found over US stocks in 1927–2007 that momentum is concentrated in stocks whose past-year move came in many small daily steps (low "information discreteness", ID): over six months after formation it fell from 5.94% (t 4.63) among the most continuous fifth to −2.07% among the most discrete, a spread of 8.01% (t 8.54), with no later reversal. Goyal, Jegadeesh & Subrahmanyam (2025, *Review of Finance*) found "remarkably robust" support for it in 49 markets outside the US over 1993–2020, but their own US table (unverified) shows nothing after 1993 (t −0.58), when momentum itself was insignificant. Here it fails. The continuous-information book (FIP: continuous winners minus continuous losers, rebuilt every 21 trading days) lost 0.71% a month net over 2006–2019 (t −1.05, where t ≥ 2 was needed; FF5 + UMD alpha −1.20%, t −4.41), and trailed the discrete book by 0.48% a month (t −1.30). Within winners the continuous ones did worse than the discrete ones, and within losers better: the reverse of the paper. In the paper's own 6-month terms the spread was −2.7% (t −1.6), against its +8.0%. Plain momentum lost money here too (−0.44% a month, t −0.83), and the 500 largest look the same. Since 2020 FIP has had the right sign but no significance: +0.69% a month (t 0.59) over 2020–2025-06 and +0.76% (t 0.47) in the 16 unseen months. A 6-month spread of +5.9% (t 3.9) appears in the liquid set over 2022-07..2025-06, but that is about six independent observations in a window picked for the dividend check, it is absent among the 500 largest (+1.3%, t 0.4), and the unseen months give +4.5% (t 1.3). The judge's account on the strongest continuous winners beat uniform random accounts by +3.5 points a year (se 4.3, ahead in 12 of 18 years) and volatility-matched ones by +4.8 (se 3.8), both less than plain momentum's top 20 through the same path (+4.8 and +5.4); in the unseen months it made +37.5% against +18.8% for the median random account (p 0.13). Verdict: no edge. It fails test 1 and test 2; test 3 has the right sign.

## Published claim

| Paper | Sample | What it reports |
|---|---|---|
| Da, Gurun & Warachka 2014, *RFS* | US, 1927–2007 | Momentum is concentrated in stocks with continuous information (low ID), and doesn't reverse |
| Goyal, Jegadeesh & Subrahmanyam 2025, *Review of Finance* | International, out of sample | Of the momentum explanations tested, frog in the pan gets the most robust support |

**Our test period is after Da et al.'s sample.**

## Rules (fixed before looking at results)

**Universe:** the judge-like liquid set, minus `c2eval.excluded(E, tw)`.

**Signals at formation t:**
- PRET = the 12-1 return (t−251..t−21, export closes).
- %pos and %neg = the shares of days with positive and negative daily returns over the same window.
- ID = sign(PRET) × (%neg − %pos). Low ID means continuous information.

**Book:**
- Every 21 trading days (the fixed calendar), sort into PRET fifths.
- Within the top and bottom fifths, sort by ID into thirds.
- **FIP** = continuous winners (lowest-ID third of the top fifth) minus continuous losers (lowest-ID third of the bottom fifth).
- **DIP** = the same with the highest-ID thirds (discrete).
- Equal weight, held 21 days (primary); a 6-month overlapping hold is reported. Market-relative returns, 5 bps per side.

**Alphas:** FF5 + UMD.

## Tests, in order (the verdict stops at the first failure; every number is still computed)

1. **2006–2019:** FIP's mean monthly net return. **Pass:** t ≥ 2.
2. **Continuous beats discrete:** FIP − DIP is positive with t ≥ 2.
3. **2020–2025-06:** same sign (FIP). **The 16 unseen months** (new export, once, at the end): same sign.

**Reported:** plain momentum (top fifth minus bottom fifth), and the 500 largest.

**Money test (the judge):**
- Score = PRET's percentile for stocks in the lowest-ID third of their PRET fifth, every fifth; NaN for all other stocks. The judge buys the top 20 positive scores, the strongest continuous winners.
- Graded with `rebalance=21`. Uniform and volatility-matched random accounts are both reported.

## The number for the file name

The judge's walk-forward change over random, with uniform random accounts, 0 bps (`c2eval.change_over_random`).

## Ways it can fool us

- **Momentum has been weak here.** FIP must pay on its own first (test 1).
- **ID and volatility:** continuous stocks are calmer. The volatility-matched judge line is the check.

## Results (run 2026-10-21)

A "month" below is one 21-trading-day period of the fixed calendar (export days 252 + 21k), and a period belongs to a test window by its end day. Returns are in % per period, net of 5 bps per side on turnover unless marked gross. t is the plain t of the period means for the 21-day books (the periods don't overlap), and Newey-West with 6 lags for the 6-month hold. Alphas are OLS on French's FF5 and UMD compounded over the same 21 days. "divfix" adds the shared dividend correction from 2022-07 on, to the signal and to the holding returns; it is the primary version and is identical to the uncorrected one before 2022-07.

**Plumbing check:** 5-day reversal through this folder's `save_scores` + judge path gives +11.8 points a year, 8 of 18 years beating random at p < 0.1, ahead in 12 of 18, as it must.

### Construction checks (run before any test was read) — pass

- **The signal, recomputed by a plain loop** over the closes for 300 random stock-days, matches the panel: no missing-value mismatches, largest differences 2 × 10⁻⁷ in PRET and 7 × 10⁻⁹ in ID, with and without the dividend correction.
  - The first run found one mismatch: MVIS on 2024-01-23 ended its window at exactly its starting price, so PRET was 0 up to rounding (±10⁻¹⁵) and its sign, and so ID's, came out differently in the two computations. |PRET| < 10⁻⁹ now counts as 0 (ID 0). This was fixed before any return was computed.
- **Plain momentum tracks French's UMD:** our top-fifth-minus-bottom-fifth book correlates 0.94 with UMD over the same 21-day periods in both 2006–2019 (slope 1.29) and 2020–2025-06 (slope 1.10). The sorts are momentum sorts.
- **ID sorts as intended.** Mean ID by cell, formations 2005-12..2019-12 (mean log PRET in brackets):

  | PRET fifth | ID low third (continuous) | ID middle | ID high third (discrete) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1 (losers) | −0.092 (−0.416) | −0.032 (−0.306) | +0.024 (−0.267) |
  | 2 | −0.075 (−0.049) | −0.022 (−0.051) | +0.032 (−0.052) |
  | 3 | −0.093 (+0.074) | −0.039 (+0.069) | +0.016 (+0.064) |
  | 4 | −0.113 (+0.188) | −0.059 (+0.183) | −0.004 (+0.180) |
  | 5 (winners) | −0.137 (+0.444) | −0.078 (+0.417) | −0.019 (+0.403) |

  A continuous winner rose on about 14 points more of its days than it fell. Within the extreme fifths the continuous third also has the larger move, by about 0.04 (winners) to 0.15 (losers) in log PRET.
- **Bad prints:** the |log return| > 0.5 rule dropped 0.054% of daily returns (10,343 of 19.0 million). Those are also the biggest jumps, the most "discrete" information, so the rule makes a few stocks look slightly more continuous than they were.
- **Book sizes:** cells hold a median of 70 stocks (the FIP legs average 89 names, the 500-largest legs 33). Before 2008 the liquid set has only about 300 stocks (the export's history is short), so the legs hold about 20 names and the 500-largest books equal the full ones.

### Test 1: FIP, 2006–2019 — fails

167 periods, the liquid set:

| Book | Gross | Net (t) | FF5 + UMD alpha, net (t) | UMD loading (t) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FIP** (continuous) | −0.63 | **−0.71 (t −1.05)** | **−1.20 (t −4.41)** | 1.51 (21.9) |
| DIP (discrete) | −0.13 | −0.23 (t −0.50) | −0.25 (t −1.09) | 1.07 (18.5) |
| FIP − DIP | −0.50 | −0.48 (t −1.30) | −0.95 (t −3.42) | 0.44 (6.2) |
| MOM (top − bottom fifth) | −0.39 | −0.44 (t −0.83) | −0.67 (t −3.47) | 1.26 (25.9) |

- FIP lost money where t ≥ 2 was needed. It is a leveraged momentum bet (UMD loading 1.5), and momentum itself lost money on these stocks: French's UMD made only +0.12% a period over these years, and our equal-weight version −0.39% gross.
- The alphas are strongly negative because FIP and MOM did much worse than their UMD loadings imply. Part of that is likely survivorship: the data holds only stocks that survived to 2025, which flatters past losers, the short side of every momentum book, and continuous losers, steady decliners, most of all.
- Turnover: FIP replaces 1.53 of its 4 units of position a period (7.7 bps of cost), DIP 1.91, MOM 1.09.

- **From mid-2022 to mid-2025 the paper's pattern appears in the liquid set:** continuous momentum +9.9% over six months against +4.0% for discrete, a difference of +5.9% (t 3.9). ### Published claim vs this data

The like-for-like comparison is the paper's: the cumulative winner-minus-loser return over the six months after formation, gross, by information discreteness. Da et al. split each PRET fifth into ID *fifths*, and the plan uses thirds, so our continuous-minus-discrete spread is mechanically smaller than theirs would be on the same data.

**Reading it.** Da et al.'s 8-point spread over 1927–2007 is −2.7 points here over 2006–2019 and +0.8 over 2020–2025-06. ## Decisions made while building it

All of these were made on 2026-10-21 **before any FIP, DIP or momentum return was computed or looked at**, unless a line says otherwise.

**Signals**
- **The window:** daily log returns of days t−250..t−21 from the export's closes t−251..t−21, 230 returns, so PRET and ID use exactly the same days.
- **Missing closes:** returns are taken on forward-filled closes, and a day without its own close counts as missing; the move across the gap lands on the next traded day. So a missing close loses one day, not the move.
- **Bad prints:** a daily |log return| > 0.5 is missing (the brief's rule).
- **At least 207 valid returns** (90% of 230), counting both missing closes and bad prints as missing.
- **PRET** = the sum of the valid daily log returns: the 12-1 log return over the valid days. Ranking by it equals ranking by the compounded return over those days.
- **%pos and %neg** are shares of the valid days; zero-return days count in neither but stay in the denominator.
- **PRET of exactly zero:** |PRET| < 10⁻⁹ counts as 0, so ID is 0. Made after the hand check found MVIS's round trip (see the construction checks), before any return was computed.
- **The dividend correction ('divfix', primary)** is applied to the signal as well as to the holding returns: uncorrected ex-dividend drops after 2022 are spurious negative days that raise a payer's ID. The uncorrected version is reported throughout.

**Sorts and books**
- **The calendar:** formations on the twins calendar (export days 252 + 21k), where the universe lives. Every period is exactly 21 trading days.
- **Eligible:** the liquid set at that day, with finite PRET and ID and a close on the formation day. A formation needs at least 100 eligible stocks.
- **Fifths and thirds by rank**, equal counts: fifth = ⌊5 × rank / n⌋ by PRET; within each fifth, third = ⌊3 × rank / m⌋ by ID, ties broken by row order. **Every fifth gets ID thirds** (the plan's "top and bottom fifths" for the books, the prompt's "within each PRET fifth" for the judge score and the grid).
- **The 500 largest:** the sorts are redone within the eligible stocks among the 500 largest, not taken from the full sort.
- **Holding returns:** equal weight at formation, buy and hold to the next formation, on forward-filled closes (a stock without a later close is held as cash). Holding returns aren't filtered for bad prints: the export is already repaired, and the sibling experiments don't filter them either.
- **Market-relative:** each cell's return minus the equal-weight return of all eligible stocks over the same period. The long-short books are unaffected.
- **MOM** = top fifth minus bottom fifth, equal weight per stock (all three thirds pooled).
- **Costs:** 5 bps × turnover, with turnover = Σ|new weight − drifted old weight| over both legs, each leg 1 unit of capital (a fresh book costs 2 units). Charged in the period the book is formed.
- **The 6-month hold:** Jegadeesh-Titman calendar time. Each period's return is the average of the six cohorts formed at t_{k−5}..t_k, each buy and hold since its formation. Its cost is 1/6 of the turnover between the entering cohort and the leaving one.
- **The event-time 6-month return** (the paper's view): each cohort's cumulative long-minus-short return over 126 days, gross. Added before results on the advisor's point that the paper's headline is a six-month spread.

**Statistics and windows**
- **Test windows by period end day:** 2006–2019 = periods ending 2006-01-01..2019-12-31 (167); 2020–2025-06 = ending 2020-01-01..2025-06-27 (66).
- **Test 2 uses 2006–2019,** test 1's window, and net returns.
- **Test 3's "same sign"** means FIP's mean net return is positive, the hypothesis's sign. The primary version is divfix.
- **t:** plain for the 21-day series; Newey-West with 6 lags for the overlapping 6-month series.
- **Alphas:** OLS on French's daily FF5 and UMD compounded over each period's 21 trading days.
- **The unseen months:** the new export, with every series rebuilt. Periods ending after 2025-06-27: the first runs 2025-06-13 → 2025-07-16 (it starts two weeks before the unseen months), the last ends 2026-10-15. 16 periods.

**The judge**
- **The score is computed every day,** not only on the calendar, because the judge rebalances on its own 21-day schedule from each holdout's start. At day d: PRET and ID at d (closes through d−21), the universe at the latest calendar day at or before d, a close on d; the same sorts; score = PRET's percentile (rank + 1) / n among the eligible stocks for the low-ID third of every fifth, NaN otherwise. Checked: on calendar days the scored set equals the books' low-ID thirds, and the top 20 scores all sit in the continuous-winner cell.
- **The divfix score is primary;** the uncorrected one is reported.

**Made after seeing results**
- **The plain-momentum baseline through the judge** (score = PRET percentile for every eligible stock), following the README's rule that a result should beat its nearest known effect. Reported, not a test.
- **The by-year table, the factor loadings and the 2006–2019 means without 2009,** to explain test 1. Reported, not tests.
- **The unseen row of the event-time 6-month column** first took cohorts by the end of their six months, which let cohorts formed in Dec 2024–Apr 2025, mostly on seen data, into it (FIP − DIP −1.9%, t −0.3). It now takes only cohorts formed from 2025-06-13 on (+4.5%, t 1.3). A relabeling of a reported number, made after the unseen run.
- **Nothing else in the rules or the code was changed after results were seen.**

**Data notes**
- Before 2008 the liquid set holds only about 300 stocks, because most of the export's stocks enter in 2005; the legs hold about 20 names then.
- The data holds survivors only (to 2025 in the old export). That flatters past losers, which every momentum book shorts, and it may be why momentum's alphas are so negative here.
