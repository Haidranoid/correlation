# Twin momentum: does the momentum of a stock's own story persist?

**TL;DR:** Does a stock's past-year gain beyond what its tracking fund explains (XLE for XOM) keep going? Not here: among the 500 most-traded stocks, a fund-hedged long-short on it made +0.12% a month before costs over 2006–2019 (t 0.33; 2.5 needed), and its top 20 trailed SPY by 0.1 points a year after costs. The hedge cut swings and the 2009 crash, but hedging ordinary momentum picks does as well. Verdict: no edge.

## Nearest prior work

| Work | What it found |
|---|---|
| Blitz, Huij & Martens 2011, *J. Empirical Finance*, "Residual momentum" | Momentum in Fama-French residuals has about half the volatility of plain momentum at similar returns, and smaller crashes |
| Gutierrez & Pirinsky 2007 | Momentum in firm-specific returns persists, unlike in abnormal returns relative to the market |
| Moskowitz & Grinblatt 1999 | Industry momentum explains part of stock momentum |
| `+2.1-momentum-12-1.md` | This project: plain 12-1 momentum matches SPY after costs; no edge |

**Novelty check (2026-10-21, a separate search; it changed no rules):** partly done, with clear US decay.
- **Blitz, Huij & Martens 2011:** residual momentum roughly doubles the risk-adjusted profit.
- **US evidence since:** Blitz, Hanauer & Vidojevic 2020 (weakening after the early 2000s), Huij & Lansdorp 2017 (unattractive in the US over 2009–15), and Hanauer & Windmüller 2023 (neither raw nor residual momentum works well in recent US data).
- **Avellaneda & Lee 2010:** sector-ETF residuals, but as short-term mean reversion.
- **Not found:** 12-1 momentum against each stock's best-tracking ETF, hedged with it.

## Rules (fixed before looking at results)

**Universe:** the 500 most-traded stocks (median dollar volume over 63 days), price ≥ $5, with a twin, minus `c2eval.excluded(E, tw)`. Refreshed every 21 trading days on a fixed calendar.

**Signal:**
- the residual log return against the twin from day t−251 to t−21: `c2eval.twin_residual(E, tw, 231, scaled=True, max_missing=12)` read at day t−21;
- `max_missing` ≈ 5% of the window, because the default of 1 would drop most stocks over 231 days.

**Book:**
- Monthly: long the top 10%, short the bottom 10%. Each stock is paired with −β × its twin (β and twin from the latest rebalance), so the book is hedged.
- Equal weight, held 21 days. Costs 5 bps per side on stocks, 1 bp on funds.

**Baselines (same universe and calendar):**
- **(a) plain 12-1 momentum:** unhedged decile long-short;
- **(b) market-residual momentum:** the residual against SPY at the stock's market beta, hedged with SPY.

## Tests, in order (the verdict stops at the first failure; every number is still computed)

1. **2006–2019:** the twin book's mean monthly return. **Pass:** t ≥ 2.5 gross, and positive net.
2. **Better risk-adjusted than plain momentum:** Sharpe(twin) > Sharpe(a), with a bootstrap p < 0.10. Also report the worst months (2009).
3. **2020–2025-06:** same sign. The 16 unseen months (new export, once, at the end): same sign.

**Money test (the judge):**
- Scores = the signal every day, graded with `rebalance=21`.
- Uniform and volatility-matched random accounts are both reported.

## The number for the file name

The judge's walk-forward change over random, with uniform random accounts, 0 bps (`c2eval.change_over_random`).

## Ways it can fool us

- **Twins change over the year:** the residual uses the twin chosen at the latest rebalance for the whole window. A stock that switched twins mid-year has a slightly mixed residual. That is accepted, and stated.
- **Survivor-only data:** the losers it shorts are survivors, which works against the short side.

## Results (run 2026-10-21)

All returns are actual returns, from closes. A long-short is $1 of stock long and $1 short. "% a month" is per 21-trading-day hold on the twins' fixed calendar, so a "month" is a hold labeled by its formation date, not a calendar month. Sharpe ratios are annualized (12 holds a year).

**The signal in plain words.** It measures how far the stock outran or lagged its twin fund over the 11 months ending a month ago, after allowing for how strongly it usually moves with that fund. It is measured in units of the stock's typical own move over such a stretch.
- Concretely, it is the stock's log return from close t−252 to close t−21, minus β × the twin's log return over the same days, divided by resid_sd × √231. resid_sd is the daily sd of the stock's residual against the twin over the 252 days the twin was chosen on.
- A value of +2 means the stock's own part rose twice as much as its own day-to-day noise would typically carry it over 231 days.
- The scaling ranks a steady own-story climber above a volatile stock with the same raw residual.

### The universe and its twins

- **Size:** the universe is always 500 from 2006-02. The ranked set, the stocks with every signal available, is 494–500 (mean 499).
- **Coverage:** of the plain 500 most-traded stocks, on average 6.7 lack a twin and 1.9 are excluded by `c2eval.excluded`.
- **Twin quality:** the median twin correlation in the universe is 0.53–0.83 (mean 0.71), with 49–136 distinct twins. Broad index funds are 20% of twins on average: 28–38% a year over 2006–2010 (up to 43% at one rebalance), and 8–10% in 2022–2025.
- **The signal's twin is not always the hedge's twin.** The signal is read at t−21, so it uses the twin chosen a rebalance earlier than the hedge's. For 20% of ranked stocks on average (11–52% by rebalance) the two differ, often between near-equivalent funds (for example VTI and IVV). This is the "twins change" caveat, accepted as planned.
- **Agreement between signals:** the twin signal's cross-sectional rank correlation is 0.78 with plain momentum and 0.84 with market-residual momentum. The books share 51% of their long picks and 57% of their short picks with plain momentum.

### What does the work: the residual signal or the twin hedge

Over all 2006-03..2025-05 (231 holds):

| | Unhedged | Twin-hedged |
|---|---|---|
| **Plain 12-1 signal** | (a): Sharpe +0.18, sd 9.5%, worst hold −44.6% | Sharpe **+0.23**, sd 4.9%, worst −21.6% |
| **Twin-residual signal** | Sharpe +0.08, sd 5.9%, worst −26.9% | the plan: Sharpe **+0.23**, sd 5.0%, worst −28.4% |

- **Both cut risk.** Hedging plain momentum's picks with their twins halves its sd. The twin-residual picks are calmer even unhedged.
- **The residual signal adds nothing to the hedge.**
  - Over the whole period the plan and hedged plain momentum have the same Sharpe; the monthly difference is +0.00% (t +0.01).
  - Over 2006–2019 the plan was ahead (+0.21% a month, t +1.39, bootstrap p 0.13). Over 2020–2025 it was behind (−0.54%, t −1.22).
- **Scaling barely matters:** the unscaled twin residual gave +0.06% a month over 2006–2019 (t +0.16), +1.46% over 2020..2025-05 (t +1.91) and a Sharpe of +0.29 overall.

### Verdict

- **Fails Test 1:** +0.12% a month over 2006-03..2019, t +0.33 against 2.5.
- **Fails Test 2:** Sharpe 0.09 against −0.02 for plain momentum, bootstrap p 0.28.
- **Test 3 holds the sign:** 2020..2025-05 at t +1.27, the unseen months at t +1.78. Neither is significant, and plain momentum earned as much in both.
- **What the twin delivers is risk control, again.** Monthly swings are 40–60% below plain momentum's, and the 2009 crash is smaller (−58% against −88% over Mar–May 2009), but not avoided. Hedging plain momentum's own picks with their twins does as well, so the residual signal itself adds nothing.
- **In these 500 large caps since 2006, 12-1 momentum has little mean return to keep.** That fits the project's lesson that same-day co-movement is useful for risk, not returns.

## Decisions made while building it

All made **before seeing any results**: before any portfolio return or judge output was computed, unless marked otherwise. No rule was changed after results were seen.

- **The signal is read literally.** `c2eval.twin_residual(E, tw, 231, scaled=True, max_missing=12)` is evaluated at day t−21 and used for the decision at the close of t.
  - That function uses the twin, β and resid_sd of the latest rebalance at or before t−21, which is one rebalance before the hedge's. The hedge uses the twin and β of the rebalance at t, as the plan says.
  - For 20% of ranked stocks on average the two twins differ. This was measured from coverage, before any returns.
  - The window is the daily log returns of days t−251..t−21 (close t−252 to close t−21), the same window as 12-1 momentum. Checked by hand in `out/checks.txt`.
- **Plain 12-1 momentum** is `momentum_scores(close, 21, 252)`, imported read-only from `stockai/stockai/baselines/momentum.py` and recomputed on the export.
  - On the raw closes it reproduces `stockai/runs/poc/baselines/momentum_12_1.npy` exactly through 2024-06-26: 15.4M cells, a largest difference of 0, and no cell finite in only one of them.
  - The experiment uses the gap-filled closes (next item). They change 3,816 of 17.0M cells, none on a rebalance day.
- **One market-wide data gap** (2011-02-17: closes for 571 of about 2,530 stocks and 67 of about 690 funds) is forward-filled from the previous close in both stocks' and funds' closes. This uses only past data, as in twin reversal. The twins themselves are c2eval's, chosen on the unfilled data. With `max_missing=12` the gap would not blank the signal; filling keeps the two-day move instead of dropping it.
- **Baseline (b), market-residual momentum,** is scaled like the twin signal, as Blitz et al. standardize residual momentum.
  - It is built by passing a SPY-only "twin" table through the same `c2eval.twin_residual`: each stock's β to SPY and the sd of its daily residual. Both are estimated at each rebalance over the trailing 252 days, the way `c2eval.twins` estimates a twin's β.
  - Like the twin signal, it is read at t−21 (β from the previous rebalance). The SPY hedge uses β at t.
  - Unscaled versions of both residual signals are diagnostics. Test 2 compares only against (a), so this choice cannot change the verdict.
- **Universe order:** price ≥ $5, a twin at the rebalance, and not `c2eval.excluded` (the fund-like flag plus `not_common`) are applied first. Then the 500 with the highest 63-day median dollar volume are taken, so the universe is always 500.
- **Ranked set:** each month every strategy ranks the same stocks: universe members whose twin, plain and market-residual signals (and the two unscaled diagnostics) are all finite at the close. Each leg is round(10% × n), normally 50.
- **Calendar and periods:**
  - The book rebalances on `c2eval.twins`' own calendar (export days 252, 273, …), so a stock's twin, β and the universe stay fixed through each hold.
  - **Test 1's start** is the first 2006 rebalance with a full universe, more than one distinct twin, SPY betas, and at least 450 ranked stocks. That is 2006-03-03, chosen from coverage alone. The 2006-02-01 rebalance had a 500-stock universe but no ranked stocks. Its signals are read at the previous rebalance, 2005-12-30, when only about 305 stocks had a twin (all QQQ) and SPY had too little history in the export for market betas.
  - "2006–2019" is therefore 2006-03..2019-12 (166 holds). Test 3's old part runs from the first 2020 formation to the last whose hold ends inside the old export (formed 2025-05-14, 65 holds).
- **Returns:**
  - Buy-and-hold from close t to close t+21, equal weight at formation, from forward-filled closes.
  - A long stock carries −β × its twin, a short one +β × its twin, also bought and held. A fund's daily log move beyond ±50% counts as zero.
  - Plain momentum is unhedged.
  - The monthly long-short is the mean hedged long leg minus the mean hedged short leg.
- **Costs:**
  - Charged on turnover at each rebalance, against the previous month's positions as they drifted.
  - Fund positions are netted across stocks.
  - Stocks pay 5 bps per side, funds 1 bp. The first month pays for building the book.
- **t-statistics:** plain t of the non-overlapping 21-day hold returns.
- **Test 2's Sharpe is gross** for pass/fail, matching Test 1's gross t; net is reported. Sharpe = mean / sd × √12.
  - The bootstrap is a paired circular block bootstrap of the (twin, plain) hold pairs, with blocks of 6 holds, 10,000 draws and a fixed seed.
  - Its statistic is Sharpe(twin) − Sharpe(plain). The one-sided p, centered, is (1 + #{d* − d ≥ d}) / 10,001, with a 90% percentile interval. Blocks of 1 and 12 are reported as checks.
- **Test 3's "same sign"** means the plan's direction: positive, so momentum pays. It does not mean Test 1's realized sign.
- **Worst months:** with 21-day holds on a fixed calendar, a "month" is a hold labeled by its formation date. Drawdowns are on the cumulative sum of hold returns.
- **Diagnostics,** not used for pass/fail: the 2×2 of signal (twin residual or plain) by hedge (twin or none), to separate what the signal does from what the hedge does, and the two unscaled residuals.
- **Money-test scores:**
  - The twin signal on every day d (the scaled twin residual ending at d−21), for the universe of the latest rebalance at or before d. NaN elsewhere and before 2006-03-03.
  - Plain momentum is scored the same way on the same universe, NaN only where its own signal is.
- **Unseen months:**
  - The same calendar on the new export, which also starts 2001-12-31, so the day indices match. `twins(E_new)` comes from c2eval's cache.
  - The holds counted are those formed on or after 2025-06-27 that end by 2026-10-19: 15 holds, formed 2025-07-16..2026-09-16. The plan's "16 unseen months" are 15 complete 21-day holds here.
  - The book starts in 2025-01, so the first unseen hold pays real turnover.
  - The judge covers the whole of 2025-06-30..2026-10-19.
