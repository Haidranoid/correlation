# stockGPT picking the biggest gainers two months out

**TL;DR:** Can stockGPT, the project's small language-model-style network, pick the stocks that will rise most over the next two months? No. Over 78 periods from 2010 to 2023, its top 10 trailed the average stock by 0.06 points a period (t = −0.1) and never caught one of the period's 10 biggest gainers. After costs of 5 basis points a trade it trailed SPY by 3.0 points a year. Verdict: no edge.

- **How it was built:** the same transformer, inputs and procedure as at one week and one month, with gaps of at least 42 trading days.
- **Target and grading:** stockGPT (`-19.8-stockgpt-next-day.md`) was retrained to predict each stock's rank by its return over the next 42 trading days, then graded exactly like LightGBM in the two-month test (`+3.6-two-month-top10-picks.md`).
- **Its picks are calm,** as at one week and one month.
- **SPY:** $10,000 became $65,036 over the same 14 years.
- **Why the null looks good:** 24% of its picks were collapsed stocks, which survivor-only data flatters.
- **The null's lead is luck:** it bought collapsed stocks in a stretch when they rose.
- **The noisiest horizon:** only 78 periods.
