# Trend following across asset classes: does time-series momentum still pay?

**TL;DR:** Does trend following, buying funds that rose over the past year and shorting those that fell, still pay? Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen's rule, run on 23 asset-class funds, earned +1.2 points a year after costs over 2008–2025, but its 2008–2019 test isn't significant (t 1.47; needs 2). It still gains in crises: +5.6% in 2008 and +6.5% in 2022, when SPY lost 38% and 20%. Verdict: works as a crisis hedge, not for returns.

## Published claim

| Paper | Sample | What it reports |
|---|---|---|
| Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012, *JFE* | 58 futures, 1985–2009 | The sign of the past 12-month excess return predicts the next month; a diversified, volatility-scaled portfolio has large risk-adjusted returns and does best in extreme markets |
| Hurst, Ooi & Pedersen 2017, *JPM* | 1880–2016 | Positive returns in every decade; positive in most of the worst 60/40 drawdowns |

**Our test period, 2008–2026, is after both samples, except the overlap with Moskowitz et al.'s 2008–2009.**

## Rules (fixed before looking at results)

**Funds** (fixed now from the store's start dates):

| Kind | Funds |
|---|---|
| Equities | SPY, IWM, EFA, EEM, EWJ, VGK, FXI |
| Bonds | TLT, IEF, LQD, HYG, TIP, EMB |
| Commodities | GLD, SLV, USO, UNG, DBC, DBA |
| Currencies | UUP, FXE, FXY |
| Real estate | VNQ |

A fund enters once it has 12 months of history.

**Prices:**
- Yahoo's dividend-adjusted closes (the shared setup), because the store stopped adjusting for dividends in late 2022, and bond funds pay 3–8% a year.
- Excess return = the fund's daily return minus the daily T-bill rate (the DGS3MO CSV in `research/lazy_prices/`).

**Signal:** at each month-end, the sign of the fund's past 12-month excess return.

**Size:**
- Each fund is scaled to 10% annualized ex-ante volatility, from an exponentially weighted variance of daily excess returns with a center of mass of 60 days (Moskowitz et al.'s rule).
- The portfolio holds the average of the active funds' positions: weight = sign × (10% ÷ σ) ÷ N.
- Rebalanced monthly at month-end.

**Costs:** 2 bps per side on turnover.

**Fidelity check, done before any test is read:**
- The monthly correlation with AQR's TSMOM factor series over the overlapping months must be at least 0.5.
- If it isn't, look for bugs and fix only bugs, against the published rule, and record what changed. Futures and funds differ, so a perfect match isn't expected.

## Tests, in order (the verdict stops at the first failure; every number is still computed)

1. **2008–2019:** the portfolio's mean monthly excess return net of costs. **Pass:** t ≥ 2.
2. **Crisis behaviour:** positive in 2008 and 2022, the two worst years for stocks and bonds in the sample.
3. **2020–2025-06:** same sign. **The 16 unseen months** (new store, once, at the end): same sign.

**Reported:**
- Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, and correlation with SPY and with a 60/40 SPY/IEF mix;
- the contribution of each kind of fund;
- the fidelity correlation.

## The number for the file name

Points of yearly return above the median of 1,000 random-sign portfolios, 2008–2025-06, at 0 bps. A random-sign portfolio uses the same funds, scaling and rebalancing, with each fund-month's long or short drawn at random. It is a portfolio of funds against random portfolios, not the judge's 20-stock account.

## Ways it can fool us

- **Few independent bets:** 23 funds, many correlated, and about 18 years.
- **2008 dominates trend following's record.** Results are reported with and without it.
- **Leverage:** bond funds scaled to 10% volatility can need more than 1× gross. Gross exposure is reported.

## Results (run 2026-10-21)

**Plumbing check:** 5-day reversal through this folder's scores-and-judge path gives +11.8 points a year, ahead in 12 of 18 years and beating random at p < 0.1 in 8 of 18, as it should (`sanity_reversal.txt`). The plan has no judge test; this only confirms the wiring.

**Checks before any outcome was read** (`checks.txt`):
- The sign of the 12-month excess return agrees with a second, naive computation (simple 12-month return against compounded T-bills, month-ends found by calendar arithmetic) on all 5,642 fund-month-ends.
- EWMA volatility looks right. SPY was 8.4% at the end of 2006, 55% at the end of 2008, 6.3% at the end of 2017 and 50% in March 2020. IEF ran at 3.7–11.7%.
- Funds enter on time: 13 in 2006, 16 in 2007, 21 on average in 2008 (EMB last, from 2009-01), and 23 from 2009.
- Months with no sign change and no entry turn over a median 4% of capital (volatility drift only), against 26% in other months.
- **SLV fell 34% and GLD 11% on 2026-01-30** (in the unseen months). The raw closes show the same drop, so it is a real price move and is kept.

- **Returns are the account's:** T-bills plus the overlay, compounded. Random signs use seed 20261021 (`numpy.random.default_rng`), drawn independently for each fund and month.

## Decisions made while building it

All made before any strategy return was computed, unless marked otherwise.

- **Month-ends:** the last date of each calendar month in the union calendar of the 23 funds' Yahoo dates (the funds trade on the same calendar, with no gaps inside any fund's history).
- **12-month excess return:** log(P_m ÷ P_{m−12}) minus the sum of log(1 + rf) over the days in between. The sign is the same as the simple 12-month return against compounded T-bills, and was checked against it. rf is DGS3MO ÷ 100 ÷ 252 per trading day, through `pubdata.rf_daily`, which reads the same FRED CSV as `research/lazy_prices/`.
- **"12 months of history":** a fund is active at month-end m if it has a close at month-ends m and m−12. EMB, the last, is held from 2009-01.
- **Volatility:**
  - Moskowitz et al.'s equation (1): exponentially weighted, δ = 60/61, demeaned by the exponentially weighted mean, on daily excess returns through the month-end close;
  - weights are normalized over each fund's own history (the history starts at the first bar);
  - it is annualized with 252 trading days, not their 261 weekdays, since the funds' returns are on trading days. The choice scales every weight by the same 1.8% and changes no t or Sharpe ratio.
- **Returns:** buy and hold within the month. A position earns w × (fund return − the month's compounded T-bill), and the account earns T-bills plus the overlay. Tests 1 and 3 use the overlay (excess return).
- **Costs:** 2 bps × Σ|w_new − w_drifted| at each month-end, where w_drifted is last month's weight after the month's moves. Costs are charged to the month the new weights are held. The strategy runs continuously from 1994-02 (SPY alone at first), so no test window starts with an artificial set-up trade.
- **Test 1's t** is the plain (iid) t of the monthly mean, with Newey-West (6 lags) reported beside it.
- **Test 2:** compounded calendar-year net excess return. The sum of months gives the same signs.
- **Test 3:** "same sign" means positive, the published sign. For the unseen months it means the compounded net excess return over the whole period.
- **Fidelity:**
  - Gross (0 bps) returns, joined to AQR's series on (year, month), since pubdata dates AQR at calendar month-ends and ours are trading days.
  - The primary window is 2008-01..2025-06, the plan's test months before the unseen ones. Every overlapping month (from 1994-02) and 2006-01 on are reported beside it.
  - The overlap through 2026-05 includes unseen months, so it was computed only in the unseen run.
- **The number:** "points of yearly return" read as the account's compounded yearly return (T-bills plus the overlay) minus the median random-sign portfolio's, over 2008-01..2025-06 at 0 bps. The arithmetic version (12 × mean monthly difference) and the share of random portfolios at or above the strategy are reported too. Random signs are ±1 with equal odds per fund-month, on the strategy's own |weights|, seed 20261021.
- **The round's 2006–2019 row** was added for the "Published claim vs this data" table and fixed before any result. The verdict uses the plan's 2008–2019. 2006–2007 ran on 13–17 funds.
- **Max drawdown** is on monthly compounded net excess returns.
- **FF5 + UMD alphas** are reported as context (the round's rule is for stock long-shorts).
- **The unseen months:** 2025-07..2026-10, with October ending at the 2026-10-19 close. The weights at 2025-06-30 are the main run's.
- **Data:** SLV's −33.6% and GLD's −10.8% on 2026-01-30 were checked against the raw closes before the unseen run. Both show the drop, so it was kept. The plan has no bad-print rule, and no prices were filtered.
- **Web sources for the published numbers:** the *JFE* article PDF (Pedersen's NYU page) and a public copy of the *JPM* article, each in its own new folder, read with `pdftotext`. Exhibit 1 and Exhibit 6 of the *JPM* article are images and were read from rendered pages. No email address was sent.
- **After the results:**
  - the always-long benchmark (same weights, sign always +1) was added after seeing the random-sign result, to separate drift from timing;
  - AQR's 2013–2026-05 window (after the *JFE* issue) was added to `unseen.py` after the main results, before the unseen run;
  - no rule, window or threshold was changed, and no bug was found by the fidelity check.
