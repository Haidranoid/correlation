# 12-1 momentum (buy the past year's winners)

**TL;DR:** Does buying the 20 stocks with the best past-year return, skipping the last month, beat the market? Not really: over 2010–2023 it beat random picks by +4.5 points a year before costs, but in only 5 of 14 years. It trades little, so after costs it beat SPY by +2.1 points a year on average, yet compounded it trailed SPY (+288% against +550%). Verdict: roughly matches the market; no edge.

- **Census units turn it sharply negative** (census IC −0.22 monthly). Its scores correlate −0.89 with the part of tomorrow's census value that is known today (see `-4.9-lightgbm-next-day.md`). Grade it in actual returns.
- **The rule** is one of the best-known anomalies and was a rule baseline in stockai's tests: it buys the stocks with the best return over the past 12 months, skipping the most recent month.
- **Costs barely touch it,** because it trades little: +15.6% a year at 20 bps, level with SPY's +15.0%, and ahead of SPY in 7 of 14 years.
