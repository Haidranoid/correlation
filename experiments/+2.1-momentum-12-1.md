# 12-1 momentum (buy the past year's winners)

**TL;DR:** 12-1 momentum, one of the best-known anomalies, was a rule baseline in stockai's tests. It buys the stocks with the best return over the past 12 months, skipping the most recent month. Rebalanced monthly over 2010–2023, its top 20 averaged +17.7% a year against +13.2% for turnover-matched random accounts before costs, about 4.5 points more. It beat random in 5 of 14 years. Because it trades little, costs barely touch it: +15.6% a year at 20 bps, level with SPY's +15.0%, and ahead of SPY in 7 of 14 years. Compounded, though, it trails: +288% monthly, against SPY's +550%. In the monthly top-10 test its picks earned only a little more than the average stock (+0.24 points a month, t = 0.4). Verdict: a cheap-to-trade rule that roughly matches the market; no edge.
