# Positive control: short-term reversal

**TL;DR:** Can the project's test harness find a known effect, short-term reversal (buying last week's biggest losers)? Yes: the 5-day rule beat random accounts in 8 of 18 years, where chance gives about 2. It made +5.4 points a year over SPY at costs of 5 basis points a trade, but it has faded since 2021, trails SPY at 20, and failed in the 16 unseen months (p 0.41). Verdict: the harness works; reversal is real but fading.

## Rule

Score = minus the stock's own log return over the last L days. Buy the top 20, rebalanced every L days (5 → weekly, 21 → monthly), with turnover-matched random guessers as for every family.

## Results (correlation2, walk-forward 2007–2025)

| Control | Years beating random at p < 0.1 | Rank IC | Long-short in actual returns |
|---|---|---|---|
| **5-day reversal, weekly** | **8 of 18** (chance ≈ 1.8) | positive 18/18, **z = 8.96** | **4.41 bps/day gross, positive 16/18, z = 2.94** |
| 21-day reversal, monthly | 4 of 18 | positive 18/18, z = 13.04 | 0.91 bps/day, z = 1.15 |

- **Strong 2008–2021:** the long-only picks beat random at p ≈ 0.000 in seven of those years, for example +64.4% against +28.0% random in 2012–13.
- **Faded since 2021:** the long-only picks lost money in each year since, while the long-short stayed slightly positive.
- **Caution on the 21-day rule's IC:** stockai's verification showed it is mostly the census-unit artifact. About +0.071 of its +0.088 IC comes from a part of the outcome known in advance (see `-4.9-lightgbm-next-day.md`), so the money columns are the ones to read.

## Results (stockai, 2010–2023, same judge)

| | Years beating random (0 / 5 / 20 bps) | Mean yearly return (0 / 5 / 20 bps) |
|---|---|---|
| 5-day reversal, weekly | 6 / 6 / 6 (7 / 7 / 7 against volatility-matched accounts) | +27.9% / +22.0% / +5.8% |
| 5-day reversal, daily | 10 / 10 / 9 | +28.0% / +14.8% / −17.1% |
| SPY | | +15.0% |

- Compounded weekly: +1,315% at 0 bps, +628% at 5 bps, −1% at 20 bps.
- It lost to the random median in each of the last three years.
- It is still one of the best simple rules in stockai's tests.

## The 16 unseen months (2025-06-30..2026-10-19)

- **Picks:** +23.0% against a random median of +19.3% (p = 0.41).
- **Long-short:** 10.15 bps/day gross, t = 0.95, with 85% of the book turned over each week.
- **Not significant.**
