# Does anything outside the stock market lead US stocks?

**TL;DR:** Does anything outside the stock market, such as weather, crypto or news, move first with US stocks following? No: across 1,753 outside series, strong correlations of that kind turned up 4% more often than chance, exactly what 220 pure-noise series scored, and links found in one 5-year period kept their direction in the next 50.7% of the time, a coin flip. The test doesn't trade, so it has no after-cost return. Verdict: nothing leads.

## Method

- **The census's own machinery:**
  - move lengths and offsets;
  - non-overlapping observations;
  - the sign-flip null;
  - the false-discovery rule;
  - a placebo;
  - the census's stock universes.

  It reproduced 350 stored census correlations to 3e-8.
- **Timing:** each observation counts only from the day it was public by the 4 pm close, a conservative lag per series. Weather counts from the next day; monthly releases from their worst historical delay.
- **Seasonal series** become anomalies against earlier years only. Everything is standardized over a trailing year.
- **Annual series and finite-life contracts** are reported separately.
- **Noise baseline:** 220 pure-noise series through the same pipeline.

## Results

| Direction, predictive offsets | Real/chance at \|z\| ≥ 4 | Noise baseline | Same sign next 5-year period | Picks from the null |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| outside data → stocks | 1.04 | 1.04 | 50.7% (n = 292,506) | 50.1% |
| stocks → outside data | 1.05 | 1.06 | | |

**By category:** 0.81–1.10 everywhere (news 0.92, politics 1.02, prosperity 1.05, Google Trends 1.03). The two exceptions are explained:
- climate indices (1.27): two near-duplicate weekly Niño series;
- monthly population (1.59): a calendar alignment with a monthly-paying fund.

**Prediction markets** have their own test (252-day periods, April 2022 to April 2025): 1.03× chance, and 50.4% keep their sign. That matches crypto (1.05×), daily Trends (1.00×) and noise (1.01×).

**The controls pass:**
- **Same-day links show up:** oil → energy stocks z +7.6 to +32.7; gold → miners +24.9 to +52.2.
- **Known next-day links show up:** SPY → Asian indices next day r +0.15 to +0.54.
- **Synthetic leads** are found at their exact offsets.
- **Placebos look like chance.**

**What survives is mechanical:**
- US-listed ETFs lead thin closed-end funds and small banks by a day (stale prices).
- Fixed-calendar releases line up with funds' and companies' payout dates. Moon phase lines up with dividend dates the same way.
- In plain terms these are flat. GRMN rose 52.5% of the time after the income series' top quarter, 55.0% after its bottom quarter, and 53.0% normally.
