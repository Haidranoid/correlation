# Outside data as extra model inputs, one month

**TL;DR:** Do outside data series, such as economic statistics and daylight hours, help a model pick next month's 10 best stocks? No, they hurt. With them the picks beat SPY by +10.9 points a year after costs of 5 basis points a trade, against +18.6 for the same model on stock data alone, and their edge over comparable stocks fell from 2.05 to 0.79 points a month (t 1.2, not significant). Verdict: no improvement; it hurt.
