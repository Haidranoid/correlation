# Picking the week's biggest gainers (stockai-1w)

**TL;DR:** Can a LightGBM model pick next week's 10 biggest gainers? A little: the picks beat the average stock by +0.32 points a week (t 2.4), about +16 points a year, and held up among the 500 largest. That is +7.4 points a year over SPY at costs of 5 basis points a trade, but the picks turn over weekly: at 20, $10,000 became $19,052 against SPY's $65,036. Verdict: real signal, eaten by costs.

**Survivorship checks:**
- **Without collapsed stocks:** +0.17 points over comparable stocks (t = 2.0); null −0.03.
- **500 largest:** +0.25 (t = 3.1); null +0.00.
- **Collapsed share:** collapsed stocks were 29% of the picks, against 33% monthly.
- **The volatility yardstick is too lenient at a week:** the null model also beats it. Returns and comparable stocks are the better guides.
- **Against the other horizons:** it holds up under these checks better than the monthly version, which makes it the most trustworthy signal of the three horizons.

**Why it loses money:** the picks change almost completely every week, which costs about 20 points a year at 20 bps. That is more than the roughly 16-point edge.
